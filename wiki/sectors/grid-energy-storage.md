## 1. 赛道定义 + 价值链

The grid & energy storage sector encompasses the infrastructure enabling the energy transition AND the AI-data-center power buildout: SiC/GaN power semiconductors, utility-scale battery storage, rare earth materials, solar generation, and grid infrastructure contractors. In 2026 the sector is sharply bifurcated — AI data center power demand is a fresh tailwind for grid buildout, while solar-adjacent names face policy headwinds from tariff uncertainty and IRA rollback concerns.

| Layer | What it does | Key players |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| SiC / GaN power semiconductors | High-efficiency power conversion for EVs, grid inverters, data centers | WOLF (Wolfspeed), NVTS (Navitas) |
| Rare earth materials | NdFeB magnets for EV motors, wind turbines | MP (MP Materials) |
| Grid-scale battery storage | Long-duration iron-air, Li-ion BESS systems | EOSE (Eos Energy), FLNC (Fluence) |
| Solar generation | Thin-film CdTe utility solar panels | FSLR (First Solar) |
| Solar inverters / microinverters | Residential & commercial solar power conversion | ENPH (Enphase), SEDG (SolarEdge) |
| Solar tracking systems | Single-axis tracker for utility solar | ARRY (Array Technologies) |
| Grid infrastructure / EPC | Electric grid construction, data center power hookups | PWR (Quanta Services) |
| Geothermal | Baseload renewable power generation | ORA (Ormat Technologies) |

## 2. 核心赛道问题

> Can AI-data-center power demand + US grid modernization drive a multi-year structural up-cycle for the power-infrastructure sub-theme (SiC + grid EPC), large enough to offset the policy/tariff drag on the clean-energy/solar sub-theme — over a 2–4 year horizon?

The sector's investability hinges on separating the two themes, not buying the umbrella. The "yes" case lives almost entirely in WOLF (SiC ramp) and PWR (grid EPC backlog).

## 3. TAM + 增长

- **Grid/T&D capex (US)**: ~$50–80B/yr utility transmission & distribution spend, accelerating with AI load — CAGR ~mid-teens for AI-driven grid connection segment. *[估算, anchored to utility capex disclosures + DOE grid programs]*
- **SiC power device market**: ~$3–4B (2025) → low-double-digit-$B by 2030, CAGR ~25–30% driven by EV 400V/800V + data center PSU adoption. *[估算, industry-consensus range; no single hard citation]*
- **Grid-scale BESS**: multi-$10B and growing, but US share pressured by Chinese cost (~$100/kWh vs US $200+).
- **Rare earth magnets**: strategic-driven (DoD), not pure-TAM; onshoring premium.

> No single authoritative TAM number ties the whole bifurcated sector together — this is a basket, sized per sub-theme above.

## 4. 生命周期阶段

**成长** — AI-power tailwind is early/mid in the adoption curve (SiC data-center PSU penetration is low single-digit %; grid EPC backlogs just inflecting). Clean-energy sub-theme is more mature/cyclical and policy-gated.

> 持有纪律：成长阶段 → hot-sector-playbook（投机）规则，非蓝筹长持。单位经济未定型的名字（WOLF、EOSE）按投机仓位管理止损。

## 5. 结构性驱动力

1. **AI data center power demand** — Hyperscalers need hundreds of MW of new grid capacity. WOLF (SiC efficiency) and PWR (grid construction) are the direct picks. Multi-year, not a one-quarter fad.
2. **Grid modernization mandate** — US transmission infrastructure is decades old; DOE grid investment programs continue regardless of IRA status.
3. **EV SiC adoption curve** — Every EV on 400V+ architecture uses SiC inverters. WOLF's automotive design-win pipeline is a multi-year backlog.
4. **Long-duration storage need** — Grid increasingly needs 8–100h storage (not just 4h Li-ion) to balance intermittent renewables. EOSE/FLNC are the plays — but execution risk is high.
5. **Rare earth supply-chain onshoring** — MP Materials benefits from bipartisan support for domestic critical-mineral production.
6. **IRA structural support intact** — IRA subsidy mechanism remains in place; grid modernization demand is real and growing (electrification, EVs, renewables integration). However, implementation pace is slower than market expected.

*Theme vs cycle:* drivers 1–3 are structural/secular; driver 4 (storage) is structural-demand but execution-cyclical; clean-energy/solar is policy-cyclical.

## 7. 价值链卡位

Profit is captured at two ends: **proprietary SiC device IP / fab yield** (WOLF — if Mohawk Valley yields hit, margin expansion is the prize; if not, value destruction) and **grid EPC backlog ownership** (PWR — toll-road on every hyperscaler substation hookup, including fossil projects).

China/competition: Chinese BESS makers (CATL, BYD) own cost leadership on Li-ion storage (~$100/kWh vs US $200+), pressuring EOSE/FLNC/ARRY. US edge is in domestic-content solar (FSLR), strategic rare-earth onshoring (MP, DoD-backed), and AI-grid EPC (PWR, domestic-only). SiC is a global race where WOLF must win on yield/scale vs Infineon/STMicro/onsemi.
