## 1. 赛道定义 + 价值链

Memory & storage chips are the foundation of AI infrastructure. HBM (High Bandwidth Memory) feeds the VRAM appetite of GPU clusters; NAND SSDs provide fast storage tiers; HDDs handle capacity storage at scale. The AI capex supercycle reignited a memory upcycle after the brutal 2022–2023 oversupply crash.

| Layer | What it does | Key players |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| DRAM / HBM | Working memory for CPUs, GPUs, AI accelerators | MU (US), SK Hynix (KR), Samsung (KR) |
| NAND Flash | Fast storage for SSDs in servers, PCs | **SNDK** (SanDisk, spun off WDC Feb 2025), Kioxia (JP, private), Samsung (KR) |
| HDD | High-capacity data center storage | STX, WDC (HDD-only post-spinoff) |
| MRAM / Emerging | Magnetoresistive RAM, next-gen non-volatile memory | MRAM (Everspin) |
| GaN / SiC Power | Power conversion for data centers and EVs | NVTS (Navitas) |
| Semiconductor Equipment | Deposition, etch, inspection tools enabling memory fabs | AMAT, LRCX, KLAC |
| Test & Measurement | Burn-in and functional testing for memory chips | INTT |

## 2. 核心赛道问题

> Can the AI-driven memory upcycle (HBM ASP premium + NAND recovery + HDD nearline renaissance) sustain a multi-year earnings supercycle for the US-investable trio (MU, SNDK, STX) — before HBM/NAND supply catches up and re-collapses ASPs (the perennial memory-cycle risk)?

This is a cyclical-commodity sector riding a genuine structural-demand inflection. The question is duration: how many quarters before supply discipline breaks.

## 3. TAM + 增长

- **DRAM market**: ~$90–100B (2025), with **HBM** the fastest segment — HBM ~$15–20B (2025) → $30B+ by 2027, CAGR ~40–50%. *[估算, industry-consensus; HBM ASP is 5–8x standard DRAM]*
- **NAND market**: ~$60–70B, recovering off 2023 trough; AI enterprise SSD demand the new vector. *[估算]*
- **HDD (nearline)**: smaller (~$15–20B) but re-rating — AI dataset capacity storage is the cheapest-per-TB tier. *[估算]*
- **Semi equipment (WFE)**: ~$100B+, memory-capex portion recovering 2025→2027.

> Aggregate addressable demand is genuinely large; the leverage is in ASP/mix (HBM, high-capacity HDD), not just units.

## 4. 生命周期阶段

**成长** — Mid-cycle of an AI-driven up-leg. HBM penetration of accelerator BOM is rising; NAND price recovery began Q3 2025 and is still ascending; HDD nearline is volume-expanding, not contracting. Not yet late-cycle (no supply-glut signal), but commodity-cyclical underneath.

> 持有纪律：成长阶段 → hot-sector-playbook（投机）规则。这是周期性商品赛道——即便结构性需求真实，也按周期顶部纪律管理（监控 NAND/DRAM 价格指数作为退出信号），非无限期长持。

## 5. 结构性驱动力

1. **AI GPU clusters demand HBM** — NVIDIA Blackwell, AMD MI300X, Google TPU all require HBM. Every new data-center build is a memory order.
2. **NAND pricing recovery** — After 2022–2023 glut, producers cut wafer starts; pricing turned in Q3 2025 and is now rising.
3. **HDD AI renaissance** — Nearline HDDs (18–32TB) are the cheapest way to store training datasets. STX/WDC volumes expanding, not contracting.
4. **Memory fab capex cycle** — SK Hynix, Samsung, Micron all building/expanding HBM fabs. Equipment orders flow to AMAT/LRCX/KLAC.
5. **On-device AI (edge)** — Smartphones/PCs with local AI models need more DRAM and NAND per device — a second demand vector.

*Theme vs cycle:* drivers 1 & 3 are the strongest structural legs; driver 2 (NAND price) and driver 4 (capex) are cyclical-recovery; commodity ASP cyclicality is the underlying risk.

- **AI HBM**: HBM is the critical accelerator bottleneck; every NVIDIA Blackwell GPU needs HBM3e. MU is the only US-listed HBM maker; SK Hynix (000660.KS) leads share but needs Korean brokerage access. HBM ASP 5–8x standard DRAM.
- **AI DC storage**: STX/WDC supply nearline HDD for hyperscaler data lakes; warm-tier HDD beats cold tape at AI access frequency. WD/Kioxia NAND adds an SSD leg.
- **Equipment**: AMAT/LRCX/KLAC benefit when memory makers reinvest; 2022–2024 capex was depressed, recovery now underway. Export controls (~25–35% China revenue) are the headwind.
- **GaN/SiC power (NVTS)**: data-center power-conversion theme; crossover with `grid-energy-storage.md`.

## 7. 价值链卡位

Profit pools by layer: **HBM** (MU — ASP premium + NVIDIA-controlled design-win allocation is the moat AND the risk); **high-capacity HDD** (STX/WDC — duopoly-ish, AI nearline re-rating volume); **pure NAND** (SNDK — 56% GM, highest-quality US NAND pure-play, + 50% Kioxia JV stake); **equipment** (AMAT/LRCX/KLAC — picks-and-shovels, non-discretionary at advanced nodes, but China-export exposed).

China/competition: US-listed names (MU, SNDK, STX, WDC) are the investable surface; the actual supply oligopoly (Samsung, SK Hynix — not US-listed) sets pricing discipline. Equipment makers carry the China-export-control overhang (~30% revenue). The structural US edge is being the *only* listed pure-plays in HBM (MU) and quality NAND (SNDK).
