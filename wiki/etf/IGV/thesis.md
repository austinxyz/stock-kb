## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

Tracks the **S&P North American Expanded Technology Software Index** — a market-cap-weighted index of US-listed companies classified as Software & Services under GICS. Passive, quarterly rebalanced. No active stock-picking; pure factor exposure to the enterprise software sector.

**One-line bet:** Own the leaders in AI-native enterprise software (CRM automation, cybersecurity platforms, cloud workflows) without stock-specific execution risk.

### Purity Assessment

**Grade: High**

9 of top 10 holdings are pure enterprise software plays. AppLovin (4.8%) is the main dilutant — it's mobile ad-tech, not traditional enterprise software, though it's a software platform by classification. ## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

AI企业软件赛道论文核心：
- AI-native workflows replacing point solutions (Salesforce Agentforce, ServiceNow AI agents)
- Subscription recurring revenue = FCF-backed, defensive in risk-off environments
- Not dependent on hardware capex cycles (unlike semiconductors)
- Enterprise AI adoption cycle: 2025-2028 platform consolidation phase

IGV captures this thesis directly through CRM (4.8%), NOW (3.9%), MSFT (8.1%), PLTR (7.7%). Cybersecurity names (PANW/CRWD) add the "security layer of AI infrastructure" angle. This is the most direct ETF expression of the thesis available.

**What it misses**: Pure-play AI inference/training software (NVDA software), Chinese enterprise software exposure.
