## Index Methodology

Tracks the **S&P North American Expanded Technology Software Index**. Selection criteria:
- Must be classified as Software & Services under GICS
- Minimum liquidity and market cap screens
- Market-cap weighted with single-stock cap around 10%
- Rebalanced quarterly (March/June/September/December)
- No ESG screens

Key difference from pure "software" ETFs: includes cybersecurity (PANW/CRWD/FTNT) and AI platforms (PLTR) which are enterprise-software-adjacent.
