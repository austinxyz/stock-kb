## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

SOXX tracks the **ICE Semiconductor Index**, a rules-based index of approximately 30 US-listed semiconductor companies. Selection criteria include minimum market cap, liquidity screens, and semiconductor industry classification. Weights are modified market-cap with single-stock caps to prevent excessive concentration. The index rebalances quarterly (March, June, September, December).

Unlike the older Philadelphia Semiconductor Index (SOX), the ICE index applies more rigorous liquidity filters and caps individual holdings more conservatively — resulting in better diversification without sacrificing purity.

**One-line bet:** Buying SOXX is a single-instrument bet on the entire US semiconductor value chain — from AI chip designers to memory manufacturers to wafer fabrication equipment makers — as the primary infrastructure layer beneath the AI compute supercycle.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

SOXX is the most direct liquid proxy for the AI semiconductor infrastructure thesis. The top holdings cover every critical layer:
- **AI compute**: NVDA (GPUs), AMD (accelerators), AVGO/MRVL (custom ASICs)
- **Memory**: MU (HBM/DRAM — the fuel for AI inference)
- **Equipment**: AMAT, KLAC, TER (the picks-and-shovels of wafer manufacturing)

What SOXX misses: pure-play photonics (POET, NVTS), which are better held as individual stocks. Also no direct TSMC exposure (fabless dominant structure).

SOXX is the best single-instrument proxy for this sector. The main alternative (SMH) has the same cost but 2.5× more NVDA concentration, making SOXX the better DCA vehicle.
