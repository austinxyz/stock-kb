## Index Methodology

**Index**: ICE Semiconductor Index
**Methodology**: Rules-based, modified market-cap weighted with concentration limits
**Selection criteria**:
- Listed on US exchange
- Classified as semiconductor company (SIC code / GICS sub-industry)
- Minimum market cap and average daily volume thresholds
- Rebalanced quarterly (March, June, September, December)

**Weighting**: Modified market-cap with single-stock caps to prevent excessive concentration. No single security can exceed approximately 8–10% at time of rebalance. This structural cap is SOXX's primary advantage over SMH (which allows NVDA to grow to ~20% between rebalances).
