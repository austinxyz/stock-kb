## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

Passive replication of the **CSI Overseas China Internet Index** — China-based internet and internet-adjacent companies listed *outside* mainland China (Hong Kong and US ADRs). Market-cap weighted with a 10% single-issuer cap, rebalanced semi-annually. The offshore-listing screen is the defining feature: it excludes A-share internet names and concentrates the fund in the ADR/H-share complex.

**One-line bet:** China's internet platform economy is structurally undervalued and its valuation discount will converge.

### Purity Assessment

**Grade: High (~96%)**

Every top-10 name is a core China internet platform. The single dilution is KE Holdings (3.9%) — an internet-native business by delivery model, but its earnings track the China property transaction cycle rather than digital consumption, so it imports a macro exposure the rest of the basket does not have.

The purity is genuine and unmatched in the peer set. **But purity is a two-way amplifier**: over the trailing 6 months KWEB returned −24.0% against FXI's −10.0% — a 14pp gap. Concentrated exposure to the thesis means concentrated exposure to the thesis being wrong.

### NAV premium data is unusable for this fund family

yfinance reports price $27.29 vs NAV $26.28 → **+3.84% premium**. Reject this number.

On the same day all four China ETFs print a same-signed premium of comparable size:

| Ticker | Prev close | yfinance NAV | Implied "premium" |
|--------|-----------|--------------|-------------------|
| KWEB | $27.00 | $26.28 | +2.74% |
| CQQQ | $51.09 | $50.08 | +2.02% |
| FXI | $35.28 | $34.64 | +1.85% |
| MCHI | $54.26 | $53.33 | +1.74% |

A fund-specific premium would not appear in four unrelated funds at once, in the same direction, at the same magnitude. The cause is **market-hours mismatch** — HK and A-share markets close hours before the US session, so the reported NAV is struck against stale underlying prices while the US-listed price keeps moving.

**Consequence for prior work**: the 2026-05-22 analysis recorded a "−2.15% NAV discount" as a KWEB feature and propagated it into `etf-reference.md`. That was almost certainly the same artifact observed on a down day. Both references have been removed. Real premium/discount requires the issuer's published daily NAV.

**Total cost of ownership** for a 12-month hold: expense ratio 0.76% + round-trip spread ~0.03% ≈ **0.79%**. Premium markup cannot be estimated from available data.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

KWEB expresses the China-internet-platform thesis more directly than any alternative. What it gives: concentrated exposure to Tencent/Alibaba/PDD/Meituan/NetEase earnings and multiple re-rating. What it misses: the semiconductor, hardware and EV complex that has carried Chinese equity returns over the past year.

It remains the best available proxy **for the thesis as stated**. The open question is whether the thesis as stated is the right one.

## 10. DCA / Entry Strategy

**Accumulation zones:**
- Primary: **$25.6–$27.2** (MA50 ±3%). Price has just exited the upper edge.
- Secondary: MA200 $31.55 is *above* current price — the deep-discount zone does not apply this cycle.

**Approach**: no accumulation while the trend template reads 1/8. This is a Watch-stage instrument; the base is unproven.

⚠️ **Trigger-design flaw carried over from the 2026-06-03 exit plan.** That plan's upgrade condition reads "reclaim MA50 **$28.87** on volume for 3 days" — it hard-codes a dollar value for a *moving* average. Two readings today:

| Reading | Result |
|---------|--------|
| Dynamic ("reclaim the MA50 line") | 2 of 3 days, no volume → **not met** |
| Fixed ("reach $28.87") | $27.29 < $28.87 → **not met** |

Both readings agree, so there is no conflict to resolve today. But the ambiguity is real and is the same defect class as **S002** (event triggers must not anchor to fixed dollar thresholds). Logged as **S007**.

**Corrected wording**: "3 consecutive closes above the *then-current* MA50, at least one at ≥1.5× average volume, with MA50 slope non-negative."

## 12. Verdict

**Watch** (upgraded from Avoid, 2026-05-22)

The upgrade rests on one fact: price closed back above MA50, which is exactly the condition the May analysis named. Directionally, it happened.

Watch and not Use, for three reasons:
1. **The condition is half-met** — 2 of 3 sessions, no volume confirmation, and a failed attempt on the same setup three weeks ago.
2. **Trend template 1/8** with MA alignment unrepaired, and the MA50 reclaim owes more to the average falling than the price rising.
3. **PW EV +7.7% trails the broad-index alternative.** Being the best tool for a thesis does not make the thesis worth funding.

**Upgrade to Use** requires all three: (a) 3 consecutive closes above MA50 with ≥1.5× volume on at least one; (b) MA50 slope turns up; (c) AUM stops bleeding.
**Downgrade to Avoid**: close below $25.6, or AUM below $3B.
