## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

MCHI tracks the MSCI China Index — a broad, market-cap-weighted index covering large- and mid-cap Chinese equities across all sectors accessible to international investors (H-shares, B-shares, Red-chips, P-chips, and US-listed China ADRs). It is passively managed, rebalanced semi-annually per standard MSCI methodology.

**One-line bet**: Broad exposure to the Chinese equity market as a whole — not a sector-specific bet. Buying MCHI expresses a view on "China's economy/market broadly," not "China's internet platforms specifically."

### Purity Assessment

**Grade**: Low-Medium

Internet/platform exposure is spread across Consumer Cyclical (Alibaba, PDD, Meituan sit here) and Communication Services (Tencent, NetEase), roughly 55% of the fund by sector label — but Consumer Cyclical itself is a mixed bag including non-internet discretionary names not broken out here. Financial Services at 19.8% is unambiguously off-theme — four of the top 10 holdings are state-owned banks/insurers (CCB, ICBC, Bank of China, Ping An) that have zero relationship to the internet-platform thesis. Combined with Healthcare/Materials/Industrials/Energy/Utilities/Real Estate (~24% more), an estimated 35-40% of MCHI's AUM is genuinely off-theme for a China-internet bet — consistent with `wiki/etf/etf-reference.md`'s existing "纯度低" (low purity) verdict.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

MCHI gives partial, diluted exposure to the China-internet thesis — you get Tencent/Alibaba/Meituan/PDD/NetEase (the core names), but roughly 35-40% of the fund's weight sits in unrelated state-bank and diversified-China exposure that neither amplifies nor hedges the internet thesis, it just dilutes it. For a position explicitly held on "纯论点/Stage 决策" (pure thesis/stage decision) grounds per the current notes, this dilution is a real cost — the position's returns will lag a cleaner internet-thesis expression like KWEB in a genuine platform-sector rally, while not offering true diversification benefits either (it's still ~all China, still correlated to the same macro/regulatory risk factors).

## 12. Verdict

**Avoid** (as a vehicle for the China-internet thesis; not a call on China-market direction generally).

MCHI is not a bad ETF in absolute terms — low fee, large AUM, reasonable (if not best-in-class) liquidity. But held against notes that explicitly say "纯论点/Stage 决策" (a pure thesis-and-technical-stage decision), it fails on both counts examined here: the thesis it's nominally expressing (China internet) is diluted by ~35-40% off-theme bank/diversified exposure, and the technical Stage read is an unconfirmed transition, not an actionable signal. The existing position should continue to be evaluated on its own facts (cost basis, account, tax situation) rather than added to — this analysis does not recommend trimming the existing MCHI position on its own (that's a separate decision outside this ETF-vehicle-selection question), only that MCHI is not the tool to reach for if adding to the China-internet thesis; KWEB already fills that role.

**Would change the verdict**: (1) MCHI's sector-weight composition shifting meaningfully toward internet/consumer names in a future rebalance, or (2) a specific need for lower-beta/lower-volatility China exposure where the bank-heavy diversification becomes a feature rather than a dilution — neither applies to the current "纯论点" holding rationale.
