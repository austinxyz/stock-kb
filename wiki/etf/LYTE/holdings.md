## Index Methodology

LYTE is **actively managed — no benchmark index**. Per the fund's prospectus description, it invests ≥80% of net assets in equity securities and financial instruments providing exposure to "Photonic and Optical Companies." Portfolio construction and rebalancing are at the discretion of Roundhill's investment team; no fixed rebalance schedule has been disclosed. The fund is explicitly **non-diversified**, meaning it may hold larger positions in fewer issuers than a diversified fund, which is consistent with the observed concentration (5 issuers each >10%).
