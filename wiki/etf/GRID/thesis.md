## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

GRID tracks the **NASDAQ Clean Edge Smart Grid & Infrastructure Index**, a rules-based, modified equal-weighted index of companies involved in smart grid technology, advanced meters, electric infrastructure, energy storage, and power management. Reconstituted semi-annually.

The index selects companies that derive a "significant portion" of revenue from smart grid-related activities — a broader definition than pure-play names, which explains the inclusion of diversified industrials (ETN, JCI) alongside pure grid plays (National Grid, ABB).

**One-line bet:** Owning GRID expresses a thesis that global electricity infrastructure investment accelerates — driven by AI data center power demand, EV charging buildout, grid modernization (US IRA), and European energy security spending.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

GRID is the best available ETF for the 电网储能 sector thesis, but with important caveats:

**What GRID captures well:**
- Grid modernization infrastructure (cables, switchgear, smart meters, power electronics)
- AI power demand enablement (data center grid infrastructure, transformers, power management)
- European energy security buildout (significant ABB, Schneider, National Grid exposure)
- Electrical contractors building the new grid (Quanta Services)

**What GRID misses:**
- Battery energy storage (no EOSE, no CATL equivalent)
- SiC power semiconductors (no WOLF, no ON Semi)
- US rare earth / materials (no MP Materials)
- Pure EV charging infrastructure (minor if any)

The individual stocks in the portfolio (WOLF, EOSE, MP) complement GRID by covering the materials/storage/SiC layer that the ETF underweights. GRID provides the broadening effect; individual stocks provide the concentrated upside on specific catalysts.
