## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

GRID tracks the **NASDAQ Clean Edge Smart Grid & Infrastructure Index**, a rules-based, modified equal-weighted index of companies involved in smart grid technology, advanced meters, electric infrastructure, energy storage, and power management. Reconstituted semi-annually (June/December).

The index selects companies that derive a "significant portion" of revenue from smart grid-related activities — a broader definition than pure-play names, which explains the inclusion of diversified industrials (ETN, JCI) alongside pure grid plays (National Grid, ABB, Prysmian).

**One-line bet:** Owning GRID expresses a thesis that global electricity infrastructure investment accelerates — driven by AI data center power demand, EV charging buildout, grid modernization (US IRA), and European energy security spending.
