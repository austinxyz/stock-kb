## Index Methodology

GRID tracks the **NASDAQ Clean Edge Smart Grid & Infrastructure Index** — a rules-based, modified equal-weighted index. Selection criteria: companies with "significant portion" of revenue from smart grid-related activities (meters, energy storage, power management, electrical infrastructure). Reconstituted semi-annually (typically June/December). Weighting: modified equal-weight with liquidity and market-cap constraints.
