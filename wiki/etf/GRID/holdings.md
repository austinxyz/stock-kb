## Purity Notes

**Why NVDA is in GRID**: NVDA earns revenue from power management ICs and data center infrastructure that is increasingly classified as "smart grid adjacent" under the index methodology. This is a stretch — NVDA's primary business is AI compute, not grid infrastructure.

**Why JCI and E.ON are adjacent rather than on-theme**: Johnson Controls' core business is HVAC and building management — it benefits from electrification trends but is not a grid infrastructure company per se. E.ON is a utility operator, not a builder — it buys and operates infrastructure but doesn't manufacture or construct it.

**The SiC/storage gap**: GRID has no direct exposure to SiC power semiconductors (WOLF, Wolfspeed; ON Semiconductor) or battery energy storage systems (EOSE, FLUENCE). These sub-themes within 电网储能 are captured by our individual stock positions, not the ETF.
