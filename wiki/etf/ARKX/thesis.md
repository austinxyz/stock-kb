## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

ARKX is an **actively managed ETF** run by ARK Invest (Cathie Wood's firm). It does not track
a passive index. The portfolio management team selects companies they believe are "leading,
enabling, or benefiting from the exploration of space." The mandate is broad, encompassing
launch vehicles, satellite networks, defense electronics, drones, AI, and adjacent technology.

Rebalance frequency: discretionary (ARK publishes daily holdings transparency).

**One-line bet:** Buying ARKX expresses a diversified bet on the space economy broadly defined
— but with heavy defense/industrial exposure diluting the pure LEO satellite thesis.

### Purity Assessment

**Grade: Low (~15-20% LEO-pure)**

Only RKLB (7.2%) is a direct LEO/launch-vehicle play. LHX (9.5%) and KTOS (6.6%) have
satellite-related divisions but derive the majority of revenue from traditional defense
(missiles, electronic warfare, drones). DE, AMD, TER, ACHR, PLTR are clearly off-theme.

Net effect: ARKX provides approximately **7% direct LEO exposure** (RKLB) plus **~10-12%
adjacent exposure** (LHX satellite comms, KTOS satellite ground systems, TRMB GPS).
This is a materially diluted sector bet compared to the LEO thesis.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

ARKX partially expresses the LEO thesis via RKLB (~7% of AUM) and defense satellite
components in LHX/KTOS (~10-12%). However, the ETF is better described as a
**space economy + defense + innovation growth basket** than a true LEO proxy.

What owning ARKX gives: Exposure to RKLB rally, defense electronics secular growth,
ARK's thematic view of space-adjacent industries (autonomous farming with DE, AI with PLTR).

What it misses: Direct exposure to satellite operators (AST SpaceMobile, Viasat), launch
economics, and LEO-native infrastructure companies.

Best available proxy? **Yes — by elimination.** No ETF provides >30% pure LEO exposure
with adequate liquidity. ARKX is the least-bad option.
