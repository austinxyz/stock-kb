## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

ARKX is an **actively managed ETF** run by ARK Invest (Cathie Wood's firm). It does not track
a passive index. The portfolio management team selects companies they believe are "leading,
enabling, or benefiting from the exploration of space." The mandate is intentionally broad —
launch vehicles, satellite networks, defense electronics, drones, AI, adjacent technology.

The ETF was **renamed in 2026** from "Space Exploration & Innovation" to "Space & Defense
Innovation" — an honest acknowledgment that the portfolio is primarily a defense+space basket
rather than a pure LEO proxy.

Rebalance frequency: discretionary (ARK publishes daily holdings transparency at ark-funds.com).

**One-line bet:** Buying ARKX expresses a diversified bet on the space economy broadly defined
— heavy defense/industrial exposure dilutes the pure LEO satellite thesis, but SPCX (SpaceX)
as #1 holding provides meaningful direct LEO anchoring.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

ARKX partially expresses the LEO thesis via SPCX (~9%) + RKLB (~6%) and defense satellite components in LHX/KTOS. The ETF is more accurately described as a **space economy + defense innovation basket** with an active management overlay.

What owning ARKX gives: SpaceX exposure via SPCX (unique — no other ETF has meaningful SpaceX weight), RKLB launch exposure, defense satellite electronics (LHX, KTOS), and ARK's thematic view of space-adjacent AI/autonomy.

What it misses: Satellite operators (AST SpaceMobile, ASTS), pure LEO infrastructure, LEO broadband plays.

Is it the best available proxy? **No longer by default — UFO is now preferred.** ARKX remains viable when UFO is unavailable or the user wants higher AUM/defense sector diversification alongside LEO exposure.
