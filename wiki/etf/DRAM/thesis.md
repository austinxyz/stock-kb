## 1. What Does This ETF Track?

DRAM tracks a Roundhill-proprietary thematic index focused exclusively on DRAM, HBM (High Bandwidth Memory), and NAND flash memory semiconductor companies. The fund is passive and rules-based — Roundhill selects companies whose primary business is memory semiconductor manufacturing, then weights them by a methodology not yet publicly disclosed. First available rebalance data is expected around Q3 2026.

The fund launched 2026-04-02 on NYSE Arca. As of 2026-05-05 it has only 22 trading days of price history. Holdings data is not available via yfinance for ETFs < 60 days old; official holdings have not yet appeared in SEC filings.

**One-line bet:** Buying DRAM expresses the thesis that AI-driven HBM and DRAM demand will drive outsized earnings growth at Micron, SK Hynix, and Samsung, outperforming broader semiconductor ETFs (SOXX, SMH).

### Purity Assessment

**Grade: High (estimated)**

MU and SK Hynix are pure DRAM/HBM companies — essentially all their revenue derives from memory semiconductors, with SK Hynix the world's leading HBM supplier. Samsung is a conglomerate but memory accounts for the majority of its semiconductor division revenue and is the primary driver of its stock price relative to the AI capex cycle. WDC is NAND-dominant and therefore partially off-theme for a DRAM-focused fund, but storage cycle alignment means it moves in sympathy.

If NVTS is actually in the fund, that's a meaningful purity dilution (~5–8% off-theme). Until Roundhill confirms holdings, grade remains estimated.

## 6. Sector Thesis Alignment

DRAM is the most direct available proxy for the AI HBM/memory investment thesis. The AI capex cycle has created an unprecedented demand surge for HBM (High Bandwidth Memory), which connects GPU clusters (NVDA H100/B200) to the compute layer. SK Hynix is essentially a monopoly supplier of HBM3E today; MU is ramping aggressively. SOXX and SMH dilute this theme with CPU, GPU, EDA, and equipment names — DRAM is pure.

What DRAM misses: semiconductor equipment (AMAT, KLAC), fabless AI chip designers (NVDA, AMD), and non-memory logic (AVGO, MRVL). If the thesis is specifically "AI memory," DRAM is the right vehicle. If the thesis is "AI semiconductor broadly," SOXX is more appropriate.

## 12. Verdict

DRAM is the most precise available expression of the AI HBM/memory semiconductor thesis — more concentrated than SOXX or SMH, with exceptional liquidity for its age. The 22-day performance (+53%) demonstrates strong institutional adoption. However, entering at $42.47 (near ATH, no MA baseline) is not consistent with risk-managed accumulation. The right action is to wait for a 10–15% pullback to $35–38 or for MA50 to be established (~July 2026), then upgrade to Use if holdings are confirmed and Chen Yun signal density supports the sector thesis.

**Conditions to upgrade to Use:**
- DRAM pulls back to $35–38 range, OR
- MA50 is established and DRAM trades within ±3% of it, AND

**Conditions to downgrade to Avoid:**
