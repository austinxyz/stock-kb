## Moat

### What DELL has
- **#1 AI server market share (20%, 2024)** and #1 overall OEM server
  revenue share (8.3%, Q3 2025)
- **5,000+ AI Factory customers** (+1,000 in Q1 FY27 alone) — Eli Lilly,
  Honeywell, Samsung named enterprise adopters
- **DFS captive financing** — lubricates >$50M enterprise deals, a real
  differentiator vs. SMCI
- **First-to-ship** NVIDIA NVL-72 GB200; new PowerRack rack-scale
  platform launched at Dell Technologies World 2026
- Investment-grade balance sheet, 40-year Michael Dell capital-allocation
  track record (LBO, EMC merger, VMware spin)

### What DELL doesn't have
- **Pricing power** — NVIDIA captures 70%+ of GPU-server economics;
  Dell's moat protects share, not margin
- **Hyperscale-deal dominance** — white-box ODMs (Quanta, Foxconn,
  Wiwynn, Pegatron) bypass OEMs in the largest hyperscaler builds
- **A cheap valuation** — at ≈26× FY27E EPS, the multiple has expanded
  well past historical hardware-OEM norms

### Verdict
**Narrow moat** — real #1 share and integration depth, but the economics
flow mostly to NVIDIA upstream and to ODMs at the largest deal sizes.
