## Business in One Line
Adobe is the creative operating system for global content production — from Photoshop to PDF to enterprise marketing analytics — now betting that its Firefly generative AI platform will expand rather than erode its dominant professional installed base.

## Asset Type
**Quality Growth (De-rated)** — Adobe is a mature, highly cash-generative software platform (89% gross margins, ~$10B FCF) that has been re-priced from 30–40× to ~11× non-GAAP forward earnings due to AI disruption fears. The valuation lens here is FCF yield (~9.5%) and non-GAAP forward P/E (10.8×) vs. historical norms — the gap between demonstrated fundamentals and sentiment overhang is the thesis.

## Moat

**What ADBE has:**
- PDF / PSD / AI / INDD format lock-in: Adobe created the industry-standard file formats; global workflows are built around them
- Creative Cloud ecosystem density: 20+ interoperable apps create compounding switching-cost stickiness
- Enterprise CC for Teams / Experience Cloud multi-year contract renewals
- Firefly licensing advantage: trained on licensed Adobe Stock content — regulatory moat vs. open AI models
- Behance + Adobe Fonts + Stock community flywheel

**What ADBE doesn't have:**
- Protection at the entry-level / student / SMB segment (Canva, Figma AI eating the bottom of the market)
- A path to re-acquire Figma (DOJ/EU blocked the $20B deal in December 2023)
- Strong social-layer network effects

**Verdict:** Wide moat in the professional / enterprise segment; medium and eroding at the consumer / student level. Durability: 5–7 years for the core professional base contingent on AI execution.
