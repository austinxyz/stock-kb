## Moat

### What OCC has
- Military/harsh-environment fiber cable specialization (DoD switching costs; approval cycles)
- Domestic manufacturing in Roanoke, VA (Buy American compliance for federal contracts)
- 40+ year distribution relationships across enterprise cable installers, 70+ countries
- Strategic backing from Lightera (Furukawa Electric subsidiary) — 7.24% equity stake + product collaboration

### What OCC doesn't have
- Scale: 200× smaller than Corning; no pricing power vs global cable giants
- Technology/IP moat: Standard structured cabling is a commodity product
- Hyperscale datacenter customer relationships: AI capex flow goes to Corning, Prysmian, Sumitomo — not OCC
- Meaningful cash buffer: $0.15M cash; no ability to invest aggressively

### Verdict
**Narrow moat** in military/specialty niche. No moat in standard enterprise cable. Lightera partnership is a meaningful capability upgrade but does not change competitive dynamics vs top-tier players.
