## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Charles Schwab exists to democratize investing — the founding insight (1971) was that the true brokerage product is the *platform and custody*, not commissions. After May Day 1975 deregulation, Schwab built the discount brokerage industry, then took that logic to its zero-commission endpoint in 2019. Today the firm monetizes the $11.8T of client trust it has accumulated over 50 years through four streams: net interest on swept cash (~49% of revenue), asset management fees (~24%), trading (~9%), and banking/advisory services (~18%).

**The 2025 transformation**: TD Ameritrade integration declared complete YE2025 (17M+ accounts converted, ~$2B annual synergy run-rate flowing). The "cash sorting" episode — when clients shifted $97B of sweep deposits into money-market funds in 2023, forcing expensive FHLB borrowings — has fully reversed. High-cost debt: $97B peak (2023) → $5.1B (YE2025). Q1 2026 confirmed structural recovery: record revenue ($6.5B, +16%), EPS $1.43 (+38%), NNA $158B (Q1 record), DARTs 9.9M (+34%), dividend +19%, $2.4B buyback executed. CFO explicitly raised FY26 EPS bar above prior $5.70–$5.80 guide.

**Current milestone status:**
- ✅ TDA integration complete (YE2025); ~$2B synergy run-rate active
- ✅ Cash sorting resolved; sweep balances growing organically (+$8B Q1 2026 sequential)
- ✅ Capital return resumed: 19% dividend hike + $2.4B Q1 buyback
- ✅ NIM normalized to 2.90% (FY2025); tracking upward in Q1 2026
- ⏳ Active-ETF monetization platform: "live by year-end 2026" (400+ asset-manager negotiations)
- ⏳ Crypto platform: rollout imminent at ~75bps/trade
- ⏳ Q2 2026 formal FY26 EPS guidance refresh: 2026-07-21 盘前
- ✅ Forge Global acquisition complete: private-markets / pre-IPO access platform

**Pivotal Investment Question:**
> Is Schwab's NII recovery (NIM trough 2.33% → 2.90% FY2025; CFO guidance 2.85–2.95% FY26; upward bias on Fed hold) the start of a multi-year earnings normalization that justifies a re-rating from 14× to 18–22× P/E — or will Federal Reserve rate cuts, Fidelity/Vanguard price competition, and zero-commission commoditization permanently cap the earnings ceiling?

**The answer hinges on**: (1) Fed terminal rate path — every 25bps cut ≈ ~$100M NII impact; (2) structural floor lift — $97B → $5.1B FHLB paydown means the next rate cycle starts from a structurally higher NIM floor; (3) monetization-layer depth — whether active-ETF, crypto, private markets, and advisory contribute real incremental EPS.
