## Critical Context

SCHW is a bank holding company / brokerage. Traditional industrial metrics (CapEx, FCF, EBITDA margins) are not the primary lens. Use:
- **P/E on forward EPS** (key: vs. 17–20× historical band)
- **NIM (Net Interest Margin)** — the primary earnings engine lever
- **Client NNA growth** — the secular asset-gathering machine
- **Capital return capacity** (buyback + dividend) — the shareholder-value engine

**Cash sorting (2023 structural anomaly):** In 2023–2024 clients shifted sweep deposits into money-market funds, forcing Schwab to borrow $97B at 5%+ in FHLB/CD funding. This ballooned the balance sheet, compressed NIM to 2.33%, and drove EPS to a trough. **Structurally resolved by YE2025** — high-cost debt reduced from ~$97B peak to $5.1B. Market still pricing SCHW as if 2023 is current — it is not.
