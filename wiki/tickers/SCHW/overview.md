## Business in One Line

Charles Schwab is the largest U.S. retail brokerage ($11.8T client assets, 46.5M accounts), monetizing client trust primarily through net interest on swept cash (49% of revenue) and escalating advisory/ETF/trading services — with a wide moat built on scale, RIA-custody dominance, and an integrated bank that no pure-play competitor can replicate.

## Moat

**What SCHW has:**
- Scale ($11.8T AUM = 6× nearest public peer IBKR); lowest per-asset tech + custody costs
- #1 RIA custodian (~$4T); advisor switching costs are quantifiably real (ACATS + operational friction)
- Integrated Schwab Bank — in-house bank funds NII engine at lower cost than competitors paying external spreads
- thinkorswim (via TDA) — best-in-class retail active-trader platform, reinforced by record 9.9M DARTs (+34% YoY)
- Brand trust: 50+ years; consistently #1 retail financial brand surveys

**What SCHW doesn't have:**
- Private-company competitor immunity: Fidelity (~$13–14T, private) can compete without quarterly earnings pressure
- Guaranteed rate-cycle protection: NIM is ~49% of revenue — Fed cut cycle directly compresses earnings
- Meaningful international revenue (~1%): geographic moat but also caps TAM to U.S. wealth pools

**Verdict: Wide and Widening** — post-TDA integration complete (YE2025) + high-cost debt $97B→$5.1B eliminated = structural floor lift that the market has not yet repriced.
