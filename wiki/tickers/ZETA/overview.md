## Moat

**What ZETA has:**
- 245M US consumer identity graph (first-party, cookie-independent data asset that compounds as clients share data back)
- NRR 128% (high switching costs once ZMP is embedded in marketing stack)
- OpenAI partnership (Athena AI agent — "superintelligent marketer")
- Forrester #1 Marketing Cloud ranking (2025)

**What ZETA doesn't have:**
- Independently verified data provenance (Culper Research allegations remain unresolved)
- International scale (US is ~94% of revenue)
- GAAP profitability history

**Verdict:** Narrow Moat — real data flywheel + switching costs, but Culper allegations create an unresolved tail risk on the core data asset's legal standing.
