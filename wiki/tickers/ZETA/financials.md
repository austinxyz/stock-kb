## 1. Critical Context

**Culper Research Short Attack (Nov 2024):** A short-seller report alleged that ZETA's 245M-identity data graph contains data obtained without proper consumer consent, including through "consent farms." The allegations remain **unresolved** — no FTC formal action has been announced, but ZETA's response has been limited to blanket denials rather than independent third-party audits. This creates a binary tail risk on the core data asset's legal standing. All financial metrics should be read with this unresolved risk in mind.

**Acquisition Revenue Quality:** FY2025 reported revenue ($1,304.7M) includes ~$101M from LiveIntent (closed Oct 2024) and partial Marigold contribution (closed Nov 2025). Organic-only FY2025 revenue is estimated at ~$1,203.5M, implying organic growth of ~20% vs the reported 30%+ headline figure. This distinction is the core of the pivotal investment question.
