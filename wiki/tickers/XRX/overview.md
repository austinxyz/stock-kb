## Business in One Line

Xerox (founded 1903) sells printers, copiers, managed print services, and IT solutions to enterprises worldwide; it added an IT-services arm via a ~$674M acquisition in FY2025, but the core printing market is in structural secular decline.

## Moat

**What XRX has:**
- Enterprise MPS multi-year service contracts (switching cost stickiness)
- XFS financing arm creates recurring relationship lock-in
- 120-year brand recognition in enterprise markets

**What XRX doesn't have:**
- Pricing power (commodity hardware, competitive IT services market)
- Balance sheet strength to invest in growth
- Technology leadership in any segment

**Verdict:** Narrow (shrinking) — service stickiness slows but cannot reverse structural print decline; moat likely erodes within 5 years without successful IT pivot.
