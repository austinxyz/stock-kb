## 1. Critical Context

**BlackBerry's fiscal year ends the last day of February** — FY2026 ended February 28, 2026. This creates confusion when comparing to calendar-year peers.

**Cylance divestiture (May 2024) distorts 3-year comparisons:** BlackBerry sold its Cylance AI cybersecurity business to Arctic Wolf for $160M in May 2024. Cylance was acquired in 2019 for $1.4B and generated ~$200M+ of annual revenue. Because Cylance was in the portfolio for the full FY2024 (ended Feb 2024), FY2024 revenue ($759.1M) is not comparable to FY2025 ($534.9M) or FY2026 ($549.1M). All analysis should treat FY2026 as the first "clean" comparable year.

**Goodwill impairments (FY2022–FY2023):** BB recognized large goodwill impairments in FY2022 and FY2023 related to Cylance and the legacy Cylance acquisition premium ($1.4B paid in 2019). These impairments make net income figures for those years extremely negative (net losses of $800M–$1.1B) despite improving operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA is a better profitability proxy for FY2022–FY2024.

**FY2026 = first GAAP profitable year:** Operating income +$50.4M (9.2% margin); net income +$53.2M — a genuine inflection that justifies analyst interest.
