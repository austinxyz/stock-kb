## Moat

**What BB has:**
- QNX RTOS: ~62% automotive RTOS market share; ISO 26262 ASIL-D certified — 3–5 year OEM requalification switching cost
- 235M+ vehicle deployments = growing installed base generating QNX royalty annuity
- NVIDIA partnership (QNX on IGX Thor for robotics/industrial/medical) — 25-year collaboration, bi-weekly executive contact
- FedRAMP High authorization — unlocks highest-security US federal contracts
- $950M+ RPO (remaining performance obligation) backlog — highest in BB history

**What BB doesn't have:**
- Proven revenue acceleration (FY2026 only +2.6%); Q1 FY2027 ~+15% YoY is promising but only one quarter
- IVY platform material revenue (commercial ramp continues, not separately disclosed)
- Analyst conviction — 6 analysts covering: 1 strong buy, 1 buy, 5 hold, 1 sell; median target $4.875
- Non-automotive QNX design wins confirmed at scale (NVIDIA partnership confirmed; named robotics/industrial customer pending)

**Verdict:** Narrow Moat — QNX safety certification and installed base create genuine automotive switching costs. Moat durability in robotics/industrial is the unverified thesis.
