## Moat

### What LASR has
- One of a small number of US-based, vertically-integrated laser diode + fiber laser manufacturers — relevant for defense customers requiring non-China-sourced components
- A $600M+-ceiling Joint Laser Weapon System (JLWS) defense contract, revenue recognition beginning Q3 2026
- Record Aerospace & Defense segment bookings (Q2 FY2026)
- Strong, recently-reinforced balance sheet — net cash ~$315M after a Q1 2026 equity raise

### What LASR doesn't have
- Scale versus IPG Photonics or Coherent — nLIGHT is the smallest of its direct laser-manufacturer peer set
- Immunity from the exact China-supply-chain dependency its "domestic defense supplier" positioning is meant to avoid — Q3 FY2026 guidance was cut explicitly citing China-linked component delays
- A track record of GAAP profitability — every fiscal year in the dataset (FY2022–FY2025) was a net loss

### Verdict
**Narrow moat.** The JLWS contract and defense qualification are real multi-year revenue locks once fully ramped, but the Q2 FY2026 guide-down exposed a genuine crack in the "domestic supply chain" narrative that underpins the defense thesis. See `thesis.md` §5 for full detail.
