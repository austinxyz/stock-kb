## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Intuit exists to simplify financial complexity for consumers and small businesses. TurboTax democratized tax filing for non-professionals; QuickBooks became the de facto operating system for small business finance. The company earns its moat through data lock-in (years of tax history, payroll records) and network effects (the entire US accountant ecosystem runs on QuickBooks formats).

The business was a consensus "AI beneficiary" until May 21, 2026, when Q3 FY26 earnings (+1.8% EPS beat) were paired with a 17% workforce reduction (3,300 jobs). Management framed it as AI-enabled efficiency. The market re-read it as: *AI is already replacing the labor that produces core user value, which means customers can get that value from AI directly without paying Intuit's premium.* The stock dropped 20% to a 52-week low.

**Pivotal Investment Question**: Will Intuit's AI integration (Intuit Assist) create more value than competitors' AI tools destroy within 24 months — or does the 17% workforce cut signal that AI has already begun replacing core user value rather than enhancing it?

*Current milestone status*: Intuit Assist launched 2024; no paid-tier penetration data disclosed as of Q3 FY26. QuickBooks and TurboTax market share data next confirmed in Q4 FY26 (Aug 20, 2026). The workforce cut is the first concrete AI disruption signal — whether it's offensive or defensive remains the open binary.

## 5. Competitive Moat

**Defensible advantages:**
- Multi-year tax data migration is operationally painful (IRS forms, state tax history, carryforward items)
- QuickBooks format is the lingua franca of US small business accounting — accountants and bookkeepers require it
- Intuit's 100M+ user tax dataset is a genuine AI training advantage if leveraged correctly
- TurboTax's IRS data integration partnerships create regulatory moat for some form types

**Moat erosion factors:**
- LLMs can now perform conversational tax guidance at the TurboTax Basic tier's level ($89 price point) for free
- IRS Direct File (free federal filing) launched 2024; now available in 25 states — directly competes with TurboTax Free
- AI-native SMB tools (Bench.co, Pilot.com, Xero AI) target QuickBooks' historical UX complexity weakness
- The layoff announcement signals management believes AI can replace ~17% of their own workforce — a double-edged sword for the product narrative

**Direct competitors by segment:**
| Segment | Competitor | AI threat level |
|---------|-----------|----------------|
| TurboTax | H&R Block, Cash App Taxes, IRS Direct File, OpenAI/Claude direct | High |
| QuickBooks | Xero, FreshBooks, Bench, Pilot, Wave | High |
| Credit Karma | Credit Sesame, NerdWallet, Experian | Medium |
| ProTax | Drake, Thomson Reuters UltraTax | Low |

**Moat Verdict**: Narrow — switching costs and accountant network remain today. Technology barrier to replication is largely gone. 3-year window is critical for Intuit to prove Intuit Assist can deepen lock-in rather than accelerate churn.

**End-market dynamics:**

## 12. BAIT Framework (Mauboussin)

### B — Behavioral

The -20% single-day crash represents an institutional capitulation event. Retail investors see "AI disruption narrative" and apply the same framework that destroyed cloud SaaS multiples in 2022. However, INTU at 13x FWD PE non-GAAP is pricing in significant permanent impairment — which may be premature if switching costs remain intact. Behavioral bias: extrapolating worst-case AI disruption from a single workforce announcement.

**Verdict**: Market is likely overreacting to the narrative signal, but the underlying uncertainty is real.

### A — Analytical

Key analytical edge: the stock market is conflating "INTU is using AI internally" (efficiency gain → margin expansion) with "AI is competing with INTU's product" (revenue risk). These are separate vectors. At 13x FWD PE, the market is pricing in significant revenue deceleration that has not yet appeared in estimates (FY26 consensus still $21.2B, +12.7%).

**Verdict**: Analytical edge exists if you separate narrative from financial trajectory — but 90-day data lag means verification takes until Aug 2026.

### I — Informational

The key informational gap: TurboTax market share post-IRS Direct File expansion and QuickBooks net subscriber data for Q4 FY26. These will not be known until Aug 20, 2026. Current public information provides no definitive signal either way.

**Verdict**: Informational disadvantage vs. insiders for the next 91 days.

### T — Technical

Stage 4. All MAs declining. New 52-week low. Zero trend template conditions met. This is the worst possible technical setup — no SEPA practitioner should enter Stage 4.

**Verdict**: Avoid. Technical component is the most actionable signal — and it says wait.
