## Moat

### What PLTR Has
- **Government clearance moat**: 20+ years of IC/DoD relationships, Top Secret clearances embedded in platform + teams; no competitor can replicate this in <5 years
- **Ontology IP**: Proprietary semantic data graph — once embedded, becomes institutional memory of the enterprise; switching cost is mission-critical
- **Apollo delivery**: Only platform deployable in classified air-gapped and sovereign cloud environments
- **AIP Boot Camp flywheel**: Rapid 5-day deployment → high conversion to contract; distribution moat growing via enterprise word-of-mouth

### What PLTR Doesn't Have
- **Hyperscaler distribution**: Microsoft, AWS, Google have billions of existing cloud customers — PLTR must compete on quality vs their quantity
- **Mid-market price point**: Minimum deal sizes ($1M+) exclude SMBs; open-source LLMs may enable cheaper alternatives for non-critical use cases
- **Brand recognition outside enterprise/gov**: Consumer-facing brand nearly zero; entirely B2B

### Verdict
**Narrow-to-Wide moat**: Wide in government (clearances + trust + classified contracts), Narrow in commercial (Ontology switching costs real but hyperscaler competition is intensifying). The 2026–2028 window is critical for establishing commercial moat depth before Microsoft Copilot / AWS Bedrock matures into a direct enterprise AI competitor.
