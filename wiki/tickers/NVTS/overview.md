## Moat

**Narrow and contested.** Navitas has genuine IP in GaNFast™ (integrated
monolithic GaN power ICs) and GeneSiC™ (SiC from the 2022 acquisition). But:

- GaN competitors: Infineon, Power Integrations, STMicro, Transphorm, EPC
- SiC competitors: Wolfspeed, onsemi, STM, Infineon — all larger, deeper pockets
- NVIDIA 800V HVDC partnership is **non-exclusive** — NVIDIA multi-sources
- Fabless model — TSMC for GaN, GlobalFoundries US GaN starting H2 2026

Verdict: **Narrow moat, not commercially validated at scale in high-power segments.**
