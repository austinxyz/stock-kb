## Critical Context — Apollo LBO Legacy

- **LBO (2016)**: Apollo Global Management took RXT private at $4.3B, loading ~$3.5B debt
- **IPO (2020)**: Listed at $21/share; current price ($7.22) is –66% from IPO
- **Apollo ownership**: ~52% of shares outstanding (~130M shares); float only 102.3M shares
- **Negative book equity**: –$1.22B (total liabilities exceed total assets by $1.2B)
- **Net leverage**: 10.7× net debt/EBITDA — among the highest in the IT services space
- Pre-FY2023 comparisons affected by impairment charges ($800M+ in FY2023–2024); normalized EBITDA is more meaningful than GAAP figures

| 2.5× | $1.87B | Would require –30% revenue decline |

**Key insight**: At 1.74× EV/Revenue, RXT is priced as if revenue will remain constant. Any revenue decline increases the implied multiple. The stock only becomes "cheap" on fundamentals if the AMD partnership drives revenue growth above $3B (requiring 12%+ growth from the FY2025 base).
