## Moat

### What RXT has
- Multi-cloud management expertise and 23+ years of managed services relationships
- OpenStack and VMware managed cloud practice (niche but loyal enterprise base)
- First-mover position on managed AI cloud services (AMD 30MW compute partnership)
- Cost reduction momentum: operating losses narrowing sharply (–$38M Q1 FY2025 → –$18M Q1 FY2026)

### What RXT doesn't have
- Pricing power: 18–19% gross margins leave virtually no buffer (hyperscalers at 55–70%)
- Revenue growth: declining every year since FY2022 peak ($3.1B → $2.7B, –14% cumulative)
- Financial flexibility: $3.19B debt with negative book equity (–$1.22B); current ratio 0.68
- Sustainable profitability: Q1 FY2026 "profit" was $8.3M net income driven by a $55.8M one-time gain; operating loss was still –$17.8M
- Customer stickiness insurance: enterprises increasingly go direct to AWS/Azure, bypassing managed service providers

### Verdict
**No durable moat.** Scale without margin is a commodity business. The AMD partnership introduces a potential new revenue stream but has not generated material revenue yet. Apollo's 52% ownership creates overhang risk. Moat verdict: **None → Emerging (conditional on AI partnership monetization)**.
