## 1. Critical Context

**Dual-currency reporting**: TSM's primary financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow) are reported in New Taiwan Dollars (TWD); the ADR trades and is quoted in USD on NYSE. All USD figures below are converted at the current spot rate (~31.92 TWD/USD) unless otherwise noted — **revenue, margin percentages, and YoY growth rates are currency-invariant and reliable**; absolute USD dollar figures will drift with FX and should be treated as approximate. **EPS figures throughout this document use Yahoo Finance's native ADR-basis actuals/estimates** (the standard US-quoted per-ADR EPS series), not figures derived from the TWD-denominated per-share statement rows, which use a different share-count convention (1 ADR ≠ 1 TWSE ordinary share) and are not directly comparable to the ADR EPS series.
