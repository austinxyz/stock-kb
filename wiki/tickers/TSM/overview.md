# TSM — Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company

**Last updated**: 2026-08-18

## Business in One Line

TSM is the world's dominant pure-play semiconductor foundry — manufacturing the leading-edge chips (including nearly every advanced AI accelerator) designed by Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, and other fabless customers, making it the structural chokepoint of the global AI-infrastructure buildout.

## Moat

**What TSM has**: 1-2 node generations of process leadership over Samsung/Intel; manufacturing yield/scale advantages built over decades; customer lock-in via co-designed process kits; control of the CoWoS advanced-packaging bottleneck that gates AI accelerator supply industry-wide; a balance sheet scale ($40B+/year self-funded CapEx) competitors cannot match.

**What TSM doesn't have**: full geographic diversification of leading-edge capacity (still Taiwan-concentrated); immunity from customer concentration; pricing power against its largest customers (NVIDIA, Apple) comparable to its leverage over competing foundries.

**Verdict**: **Wide moat**, arguably widening as the AI buildout raises the switching cost of any customer's roadmap being tied to TSM's process nodes. See thesis.md §5 for full detail.
