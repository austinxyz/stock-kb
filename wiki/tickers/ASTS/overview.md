## Business in One Line

AST SpaceMobile is building a low-Earth-orbit satellite constellation that delivers cellular broadband directly to unmodified smartphones — no special hardware required — via wholesale agreements with Tier 1 mobile carriers (AT&T, Verizon, Rakuten, Vodafone).

## Moat

**What ASTS has:**
- 3GPP-compliant architecture — works with every existing smartphone and carrier SIM card (not SpaceX's T-Mobile-exclusive original model)
- Strategic telecom investors with skin in the game: Rakuten (10.4%), Alphabet/Google (3.0%), Vodafone (1.8%)
- Commercial agreements signed with AT&T + Verizon (US); Rakuten (Japan); Vodafone (EU)
- PhRMA-style B2B2C model: carriers pay per MB, consumers see no new app or hardware

**What ASTS doesn't have:**
- Proven scale revenue (FY2025 = $71M, FY2026E = $170M — still pre-scale)
- Capital cost advantage vs SpaceX (reusable Falcon 9 enables sub-$1M/launch vs ASTS's third-party launch costs)
- Profitability path before 2028 at the earliest (FY2027 EPS still -$0.65E)
- Proven unit economics per subscriber per MB at commercial scale

**Verdict:** Narrow — 3GPP multi-carrier architecture creates switching-cost differentiation vs SpaceX's T-Mobile exclusive; durability depends on whether ASTS locks up enough Tier 1 carriers before SpaceX pivots to carrier-agnostic model.
