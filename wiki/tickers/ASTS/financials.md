## Critical Context

ASTS is a pre-profitability satellite infrastructure company in active constellation build-out. FY2025 was the first year of meaningful commercial revenue ($70.9M). All prior years reflected pure development-stage spending. Losses are structural and expected until constellation is at scale (~2028 at earliest). P/E and EV/EBITDA are meaningless; focus on revenue trajectory, cash runway, and dilution pace.

**Q4 2025 revenue ($54.3M) vs Q1 2026 ($14.7M) disparity**: Q4 likely included milestone payments from AT&T/Verizon commercial launch agreements. Q1 2026 reflects run-rate service revenue. Monitor Q2 2026 (reported 2026-08-10) for trend direction.
