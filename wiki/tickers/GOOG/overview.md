## Business in One Line

Alphabet runs the world's dominant internet advertising engine (Google Search, YouTube) plus an emerging enterprise cloud platform (Google Cloud), with AI infrastructure at the center of both offense and defense.

## Moat

**What GOOG has:**
- Search monopoly — 90%+ global share, 25+ years of ranking data, self-reinforcing query loop
- Android/Chrome distribution — 3B+ active devices; default placement for search and apps
- YouTube — #1 video platform, creator ecosystem flywheel, strong ad CPM premium
- AI research depth — Gemini, TPUs, DeepMind; infrastructure others can't replicate cheaply
- Google Cloud — enterprise contracts locking in with Workspace + AI tooling

**What GOOG doesn't have:**
- Enterprise software stickiness (Office 365 lock-in level)
- Hardware moat (Pixel/Nest are nice, not strategic)
- Protection from AI-native search interfaces eating "first query" share

**Verdict**: Wide. Search + Android distribution is a 10+ year fortress. AI Overviews buys time, but the long-term monetization question is open.
