## Moat

**What COMP has**: largest US agent network + tech platform (agent-productivity tools, brand); scale economies in a fragmented brokerage market; acquisition-driven consolidation (@properties/Christie's, Anywhere-related deals 2025-26).

**What COMP doesn't have**: brokerage is a low-margin/commoditized business; NAR commission-structure reform pressures the model; agents are mobile (retention is competitive, no switching-cost lock-in); rev growth is largely *bought* (acquisitions), not organic.

**Verdict**: **Narrow** — scale + tech edge in a structurally low-moat industry. The largest agent network and tech platform give real but limited durability; the industry's commoditization and NAR commission pressure cap the moat's width.
