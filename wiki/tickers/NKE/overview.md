## Moat

### What NKE Has
- **Brand dominance**: World's #1 sports brand; Jordan Brand is effectively a luxury sneaker franchise
- **Athlete/team IP lock-in**: LeBron, Cristiano Ronaldo, Federer-era legacy — multi-decade exclusive relationships create cultural gravity
- **Manufacturing network**: 120+ factories in Vietnam/Indonesia with years of trust and capacity priority
- **Digital ecosystem**: Nike App + SNKRS + Nike Run Club (~80M+ users) — direct consumer data moat

### What NKE Lacks
- **Running performance leadership**: HOKA and On Running (ONON) now dominate premium running — Nike ceded this sub-category under Donahoe
- **Athleisure dominance**: Lululemon owns premium women's performance/lifestyle — Nike is playing catch-up
- **Cultural freshness**: Adidas recaptured streetwear (Samba era); Nike lost creative edge 2021–2024

### Verdict
**Wide moat** — brand and endorsement IP are durable over decades. Currently eroding at edges due to 3-year strategic misstep (DTC over-rotation, wholesale partner neglect). Hill turnaround is aimed at restoring distribution and innovation. Verify FY2027.
