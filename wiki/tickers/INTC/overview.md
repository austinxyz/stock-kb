## Moat

**Narrow, contested, but historically deep.**

### What INTC has
- Only US-based high-volume logic chip manufacturer with sub-5nm capability (Intel 18A → ~2nm class)
- CHIPS Act: $8.5B direct + $11B loans — national security "must succeed" positioning
- x86 architecture installed base: 80%+ data center CPUs, near-100% PC
- IFS (Intel Foundry Services) as potential second revenue stream with external customers
- Lipbu Tan (CEO from 2025): semiconductor industry legend, deep foundry expertise (ex-Cadence CEO 16 yrs)

### What INTC doesn't have
- Process leadership: TSMC N2 shipping in volume; Intel 18A still in qualification (est. mid-2026 volume)
- Customer momentum in IFS: AWS, Apple, Qualcomm still exclusively TSMC; Intel 18A needs anchor customer public commitment
- Gross margin recovery: 39.4% (Q1 2026) vs TSMC 58%+, AMD 50%+ — Intel structurally lower due to foundry overhead
- FCF: Cumulative –$50B+ FCF burn FY2022-2025; positive FCF not expected before 2027

### Verdict
**Narrow moat in x86 installed base + unique US CHIPS position. IFS moat is unproven.** The bull case requires Intel 18A to execute yield targets and win 2–3 tier-1 IFS customers by end of 2026.
