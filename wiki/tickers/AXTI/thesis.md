## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

**Raison d'être**: Silicon moves data electronically; Indium Phosphide moves data optically. As AI training clusters scale to thousands of GPUs, the data movement bottleneck shifts from copper to fiber — and InP is the foundational substrate for the lasers and photodetectors that make high-speed optical transceivers work. AXT, Inc. manufactures the compound semiconductor substrates (InP, GaAs, Ge, InAs) that sit at the very base of this supply chain. Without InP wafers, there are no 800G/1.6T optical modules; without those modules, AI data centers cannot scale.

AXT was founded in 1986 and is one of the few commercial-scale InP substrate producers globally (alongside Sumitomo Electric, Vital Advanced Material). It controls the critical step of crystal growth — specifically the Bridgman and Vertical Gradient Freeze (VGF) methods — which require decades of process expertise to master. The company serves optical transceiver OEMs (Coherent, II-VI, Lumentum, InPhi/Marvell-ecosystem integrators) as well as 5G power amplifier customers and space solar customers.

**Strategic position as of 2026-05-07**:

### Business Model

AXT is a materials supplier — it sells substrate wafers, not chips. Revenue = volume × ASP per wafer. The economics are: high fixed-cost crystal growth operations, low marginal cost per additional wafer once the boule is grown. This means gross margins are highly operating-leverage-driven: at $141M revenue (FY2022), GM was 36.9%; at $88M revenue (FY2025), GM collapsed to 12.7%. The FY2026E $143M revenue target, if achieved, should restore GM to 25-30% range through operating leverage alone.

## 5. Competitive Moat

**What makes AXTI defensible**:
- InP crystal growth is a 30+ year accumulated expertise — crystal orientation, doping profiles, defect control. Competitors cannot replicate overnight.
- Established OEM qualifications: switching substrate suppliers requires lengthy re-qualification (6-18 months). Existing relationships are sticky.
- InP capacity is hard to build: clean-room facilities, specialized equipment, trained technicians. The capacity doubling took >12 months to execute.

**Direct competitors**:

| Competitor | InP Position | Threat |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| Sumitomo Electric | Global InP leader; Japan | High quality, premium price; limited US presence |
| Vital Advanced Material (China) | Growing InP supplier | Rapidly qualifying; lower cost |
| AXT-Dongli JV (China) | AXT's own China subsidiary | Internal; serves local China demand |
| Wafer Technology (UK) | Niche supplier | Smaller scale |

**What erodes the moat**:
- Chinese suppliers (Vital, others) are actively qualifying at OEMs. If they achieve parity on defect density and crystal uniformity, price competition will intensify significantly.
- InP is ultimately a substrate — if OEMs vertically integrate crystal growth (as some have hinted), AXTI loses leverage.
- Material substitution is a long-term risk: Silicon Photonics (SiP) reduces InP intensity per transceiver; future architectures may use less InP per unit.

**Verdict**: Narrow — moat is durable for 3-5 years given the established qualification relationships and Chinese competitor quality gap, but not structurally wide. Verify by monitoring competitor qualification progress at Coherent/LITE quarterly calls.

## 9. Industry-Specific Macro Analysis

**TAM**: Global compound semiconductor substrate market ~$2-3B (2025), growing 15-20%/year driven by AI photonics. InP specifically is ~$300-500M of that, expected to grow 30-40%/year through 2028 as 800G/1.6T transceiver deployments accelerate.

**End-market dynamics**:

| End Market | 2026 Trend | AXTI Exposure |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|
| AI datacenter optical (800G/1.6T) | 🟢 Accelerating — hyperscaler capex growing 25%+ | High (primary driver) |
| 5G/6G RF front-end | 🟡 Stable — base station upgrades ongoing | Medium |
| Space solar / CPV | 🟡 Stable/growing — LEO constellation expansion | Low-Medium |
| Defense IR | 🟡 Steady — defense budget tailwinds | Low |
| Traditional telecom | 🔴 Soft — legacy telecom capex under pressure | Declining |

**Competitive/pricing dynamics**: InP substrate pricing is negotiated directly with OEM customers, typically on annual contracts. In periods of excess supply, pricing erodes significantly (as happened FY2023-FY2025). In periods of tightness (the current environment), pricing holds or increases. AXTI's capacity doubling positions it to capture volume at current/better pricing; the risk is that they overbuild relative to actual demand.

**Policy/regulatory**: No specific AXTI regulatory risk. US CHIPS Act does not directly benefit substrate makers (focused on chip fabrication), but the broader "de-risking from Chinese supply chains" narrative benefits AXTI as a US-listed supplier.
