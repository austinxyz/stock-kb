## Business in One Line

AXT, Inc. manufactures compound semiconductor substrates — Indium Phosphide (InP), Gallium Arsenide (GaAs), Germanium (Ge), Indium Arsenide (InAs) — which are the critical enabling materials for AI data center optical interconnects, 5G, and space solar applications.

## Moat

**What AXTI has**
- InP crystal growth IP: decades of Bridgman/VGF technique refinement
- OEM qualification relationships with major optical transceiver manufacturers
- Hard-to-replicate infrastructure: doubling InP capacity takes 2+ years to build

**What AXTI doesn't have**
- No software, IP protection beyond manufacturing know-how
- No logistics moat or customer lock-in beyond switching-cost inertia
- Chinese substrates producers improving quality rapidly (Vital Advanced Material, AXT-Dongli JV)

**Verdict**: Narrow — durable for 3-5 years; erodes as Chinese competitors qualify at OEMs
