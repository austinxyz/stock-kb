## Moat

**What GLW has**: 167-year materials-science IP (proprietary fusion-glass + fiber-draw process); scale (#1 optical fiber globally); deep hyperscaler relationships with **locked capacity** (NVDA ≈$0.5B investment + Meta production-capacity commitment); **route-agnostic** — fiber is needed regardless of whether CPO or pluggable optics wins the data-center architecture war.

**What GLW doesn't have**: low-end fiber is somewhat commoditizable; cyclical Display/Environmental segments dilute the optical growth story; capital intensity keeps FCF thin ($612M) and the balance sheet leveraged ($9.9B debt).

**Verdict**: **Wide** — durable, route-agnostic. The materials-science IP + scale + locked hyperscaler capacity are hard to replicate; route-agnosticism removes the single biggest architecture risk that haunts the transceiver names.
