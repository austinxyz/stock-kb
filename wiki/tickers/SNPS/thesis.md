## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Synopsys exists because modern chips — with tens of billions of transistors — cannot be designed by hand. EDA software (electronic design automation) is the indispensable toolchain that lets semiconductor engineers design, simulate, verify, and tape out chips. Synopsys and Cadence form a duopoly controlling ~80% of the EDA market; every major chip (NVDA GPUs, AAPL silicon, etc.) is designed on their tools. The switching cost is near-absolute: a chip company's entire design methodology, IP library, and engineering workforce are built around one vendor's toolchain.

In early 2026 Synopsys closed its **~$35B acquisition of Ansys** (simulation/multiphysics CAE software), expanding from chip-level EDA into "silicon-to-systems" — covering the full design stack from transistor to the physical system the chip lives in. This transformed the balance sheet (debt $684M → $14.3B, shares 154M → 186M) and is the central integration story for 2026–2027.

**Milestone status (2026-06-01):**
- ✅ Ansys acquisition closed; consolidation drove TTM revenue to $8.68B (+41.9% YoY incl. Ansys)
- ✅ Q2 FY26 (reported 2026-05-27): EPS $3.35 beat $3.16 (+6%); 4th consecutive beat (3 of 4)
- ⏳ Ansys synergy realization: cross-sell + cost synergies expected over 1–2 years
- ⚠️ China EDA export restrictions: US curbs on EDA software sales to China — ~10–16% of revenue at risk
- ⏳ Multiple de-rating: stock -27% from $651 high on China + integration overhang

**Pivotal Investment Question:**
> Will Synopsys integrate Ansys successfully (delivering silicon-to-systems synergy) and navigate US-China EDA export restrictions (~10–16% revenue at risk) to resume mid-teens revenue growth and re-rate toward its historical 35x+ multiple — or will China revenue loss + integration friction cap it as a mid-20s P/E value trap?

## 4. Revenue Mix & Business Model

### 业务分部

| 分部 | 内容 | 占比 | 模式 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Design Automation（EDA）| 数字/定制 IC 设计、验证、仿真、FPGA、TCAD | ~65% | 时间型/订阅型许可（recurring）|
| Design IP | 预验证 IP 核（接口/处理器/存储器）| ~25% | 许可 + 版税 |
| Ansys（新增 CAE/仿真）| Multiphysics、结构/流体/电磁仿真 | ~10–15% | 订阅型许可 |

### 商业模式机制

Synopsys 主要为**订阅/时间型许可**（recurring revenue ~85%+），客户多年合约锁定，营收能见度极高。EDA 工具按席位/算力授权；Design IP 按使用 + 版税。82%+ 毛利率为软件本色。Ansys 并入后从"芯片设计"扩至"芯片 + 系统仿真"，向客户提供端到端"硅到系统"平台——交叉销售是核心协同逻辑。

## 5. Competitive Moat

### 护城河来源

| 来源 | 强度 | 证据 |
|------|------|------|
| EDA 双寡头（SNPS+CDNS ~80%）| 极强 | 全球每颗先进芯片都用其工具；第三玩家 Siemens EDA 远小 |
| 切换成本 | 极强 | 客户设计方法/IP库/工程师全绑定单一工具链，迁移成本天文数字 |
| Design IP 库 | 强 | 随设计复杂度增长；接口 IP（PCIe/HBM/UCIe）随 AI 互连暴增 |
| AI 顺风 | 强（增长中）| AI 芯片复杂度 → EDA 用量↑ + Synopsys.ai 自动化工具溢价 |
| Ansys 协同（新）| 中（待证）| 硅到系统平台扩 TAM，但协同兑现需执行 |

### 竞争格局

| 对手 | 定位 | 威胁 |
|------|------|------|
| Cadence (CDNS) | EDA 双胞胎，无大并购包袱 | 高（直接竞争，近期跑赢）|
| Siemens EDA (Mentor) | 第三玩家 | 中 |
| 中国本土 EDA（华大九天等）| 被扶持国产替代 | 中长期（中国禁令反而催化国产替代）|

### 侵蚀因素
- 中国国产 EDA 替代（美国禁令的反作用）：长期可能侵蚀中国市场份额
- AI 大模型自动芯片设计：极长期可能改变 EDA 价值，但 SNPS 自身在推 AI EDA（更可能是受益方）

**Verdict: Wide and Durable** — EDA 双寡头切换成本是科技界最强护城河之一，验证期 5–10 年。唯一真实裂缝 = 中国（禁令 + 国产替代双向），但占比 ~10–16% 非致命。
