## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

**Raison d'être**: inTEST provides specialized test and process technology
equipment to sectors requiring high-precision thermal, electrical, or
process control. It began as a pure-play semiconductor test-equipment
supplier (manipulators, docking hardware, thermal chambers for IC testing)
and has spent the last 5–7 years deliberately diversifying through
**M&A** (Alfamation 2023 for EV/electronics test; Acculogic 2022 for
flying-probe PCB test; Ambrell for induction heating in Process
Technologies) into adjacent industrial, life-sciences, defense, and
automotive/EV end markets.

**Strategic position (as of June 2026)**: Q1 2026 confirmed the FY2025
inflection — revenue $33.9M (+27.2% YoY), EPS $0.16 adj (+84.6% beat),
GM 45.5%. FY2026 guide was raised to $133.9M midpoint. The Vision 2030
diversification strategy is executing: Defense +106% YoY, Life Sciences
+112% YoY in Q1 2026. Semi orders remain weak (book-to-bill 0.73 in Q1),
and OCF turned negative (–$3.3M) due to working capital build expected to
normalize in Q2. Stock pulled back from $18.90 to a handle low of $16.82
(June 24) before today's +7.07% bounce — cup-with-handle forming with
$18.90 as the breakout pivot.

**Chen scan 2026-06-25**: INTT scored ⭐翻倍候选 #1 (highest-ranked) across

bonus), 2B=4/6 types / 3/5 formula, 2C=✅⚠️⚠️✅✅ = 3/5.

**Pivotal Question**: *Does Q2 2026 (report Aug 5, 2026) confirm order
stabilization at ≥$33M and positive OCF recovery — proving the Q1 working

## 5. Competitive Moat

### What INTT has

- **Diverse niche products** across three segments — no single competitor
  touches all three simultaneously
- **Long-lived installed base** in semi-test manipulators and thermal
  chambers — aftermarket annuity revenue in Electronic Test
- **Geographic + end-market diversification** — ~80% non-semi revenue is
  structurally unusual for a company classified as "semiconductor test"
- **Positive FCF discipline through the cycle** — rare among overlay speculatives
- **Minimal dilution** — 14–17% over 5 years vs. 5× for most NVTS/POET-type names

### What INTT lacks

- **Scale**: $114M FY25 vs. COHU $453M / FORM $785M / Teradyne ~$2.7B
- **AI leverage**: AEHR has explicit AI burn-in (35%+ of FY25 rev). COHU has
  Eclipse platform. INTT has no comparable AI-specific disclosed revenue.
- **Pricing power**: Industrial capital-equipment buyers negotiate hard; INTT
  is largely a price-taker
- **Analyst coverage depth**: Only 3 analysts; institutional sponsorship thin
  (Royce –7.9% in Q1 2026; CM Management –21.0% in Q1 2026)

### Verdict

**Narrow / Niche.** INTT has durable specialized niches and disciplined
diversification, but lacks the technology moat that would justify a premium
multiple. Fair-value multiple closer to diversified industrial small-cap
(0.8–1.5× sales) than AI-test premium (3–6× sales). Time horizon to verify
moat durability: 2–3 years of sustained >45% GM through a full cycle.

## 9. Industry-Specific Macro Analysis

### Semiconductor Test Equipment Market

- Total semi ATE market ~$6–7B annually; Teradyne (~$2.7B) and Advantest (~$4B+)
  dominate
- Growth drivers for 2026–2028: HBM/advanced packaging test, AI processor burn-in
  (SiC for power, then HBM for memory), SiC power device test
- **INTT's semi exposure** (~20% of revenue) is via Electronic Test segment's
  manipulators/docking — incumbent, not AI-new
- FormFactor (FORM) and Cohu (COHU) are cleaner AI-test plays; AEHR pivoted to
  AI GPU burn-in (35%+ of revenue — explicit AI leverage)
- INTT will NOT get re-rated toward AI-test multiples (AEHR: ~70× EV/Rev) without
  explicit AI revenue disclosure

### Auto/EV Test Market

- Battery test, electrification powertrain test — secular tailwind, but 2025–2026
  saw EV demand softness (OEM pushout, Rivian/Lucid/GM delays)
- INTT's Alfamation (Jan 2023) positioned for EV test; ~18% of Q1 revenue
- Q4 2025 Auto/EV orders softened (per management); Q1 2026 trend unclear

### Defense/Aerospace Test

- US defense capex structurally elevated (AUKUS, counter-China, supply chain
  onshoring, drone proliferation)
- INTT's thermal and test equipment used in defense qualification — highest-growth
  segment at +106% YoY Q1 2026
- Multi-year secular driver; least cyclical segment

### Life Sciences

- Biomedical freezers, thermal conditioning for pharma/biotech — stable,
  non-cyclical, recurring
- +112% YoY Q1 2026 off small base; strategic expansion vector

### Overall Macro Takeaway

INTT is **not primarily an AI-datacenter story**. It is a diversified industrial
test-equipment play with 80% non-semi revenue. The Chen "AI 受益者" framing
applies partially only to the ~20% semi segment, and even there AI-specific
exposure is less direct than AEHR/COHU/FORM. The correct valuation frame is
**diversified industrial small-cap**, not semiconductor equipment premium.
