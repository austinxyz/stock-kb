## Business in One Line

Axcelis designs and manufactures ion implantation equipment for semiconductor fabs — the specialized machines that precisely dope silicon wafers with impurity atoms, a step required by every logic, memory, and power chip on the market — and holds approximately 80% share of the fast-growing SiC power device implant segment.

## Moat

**What ACLS has**
- ~80% market share in SiC power device ion implantation — only two major global players (Axcelis, Sumitomo)
- High switching costs: ion implant process recipes are fab-specific; customers do not swap vendors mid-process
- Installed base generates recurring Claris service revenue (~30–35% of total, higher-margin)
- Deep co-development relationships with SiC leaders (STMicroelectronics, Onsemi, Wolfspeed)

**What ACLS doesn't have**
- Scale moat: 10–15× smaller than AMAT/LRCX/KLAC — cannot compete on pricing breadth or cross-platform lock-in
- Logic moat: In standard CMOS logic implant, faces stronger competition from Applied Materials
- Geographic moat: China revenue capped by export restrictions

**Verdict**: Narrow-to-Wide specifically in SiC implant; Narrow in memory/logic implant.
