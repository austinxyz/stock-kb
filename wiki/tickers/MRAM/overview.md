## Moat

### What MRAM Has
- Only high-volume public STT-MRAM manufacturer globally — no direct listed competitor
- JEDEC industrial qualification moat (12–18 month customer switching cost)
- DoD certification pipeline — $40M military contract signals multi-year approval completed
- 15+ years of MTJ process IP accumulated since Freescale spin-off (2008)

### What MRAM Doesn't Have
- Manufacturing scale (85 employees, $55M revenue)
- Pricing power vs. potential large-fab entry (Samsung/TSMC MRAM R&D active)
- Data center customer (hyperscaler win = speculative, not confirmed)
- Single-supplier risk (GlobalFoundries dependent for core production)

### Verdict
**Narrow** — defensible niche in industrial/defense for 3–5 years; long-term sustainability requires data center adoption before large fabs commercialize competing MRAM.
