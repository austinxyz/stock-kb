## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

**Raison d'être**: Magnetoresistive RAM (MRAM) occupies a unique position in the memory hierarchy: it is non-volatile like NAND flash, byte-addressable like DRAM, and has near-unlimited write endurance (~unlimited vs. ~100K cycles for flash). Everspin Technologies — spun out of Freescale Semiconductor in 2008 — is the world's **only high-volume public MRAM manufacturer**, producing Toggle MRAM and Spin-Transfer Torque MRAM (STT-MRAM) for industrial, medical, automotive, aerospace/defense, and data center markets.

The company serves three structural tailwinds: (1) replacement of battery-backed SRAM in industrial power-loss protection applications; (2) persistent memory for AI inference engines and NVDIMMs in data centers; (3) defense electronics demanding radiation-hardened, non-volatile storage that cannot be erased on power loss (DoD qualification pipeline). Everspin also licenses its Magnetic Tunnel Junction (MTJ) foundry process to third parties as a differentiated revenue stream.

**Strategic position as of 2026-05-11**:
- Revenue recovering from FY2024 trough ($50.4M) — $55.2M in FY2025, $14.9M in Q1 2026 (sequential highs)
- On **May 7, 2026**, signed a landmark **$40M agreement for Military/Aerospace MRAM applications** — equal to 73% of FY2025 annual revenue
- Investing in US manufacturing expansion ($11.1M construction-in-progress, FY2025 CapEx $8.7M vs. prior year $3.1M)
- Analyst coverage: only 2 analysts (Needham: Buy, target $18.50; 1 other: Buy $17.50). Both targets are >50% below current price
- Cash $44.5M, minimal debt ($3.3M capital leases only)

**Pivotal Question**: **Will Everspin close ≥$100M in total contracted defense and data center revenue by end of FY2027, transforming from a $55M-revenue niche supplier into a $150M-scale franchise that justifies the current $934M market cap?**

Current milestone status:

Revenue model: product sales (83%), foundry services (10%), licensing (7%). Gross margin at scale targets 55–60% (achievable if defense mix grows); currently pressured to 51% by lower volume.

## 5. Competitive Moat

### What MRAM Has
- **First-mover in STT-MRAM**: No other public company commercially manufactures high-volume STT-MRAM; all competitors are private (Avalanche Technology) or foundry-research stage (Samsung, TSMC labs)
- **JEDEC qualification**: Industrial/automotive customers require 12–18 month qualification cycles; once in, switching costs are real
- **DoD certification pipeline**: Military customers require extreme vetting; $40M deal signals a multi-year approval process has been completed
- **IP depth**: 15+ years of MTJ process learning and patent portfolio accumulated since Freescale spin-off
- **Sole-source positioning in some defense programs**: Non-volatile memory with radiation tolerance — limited alternatives

### What MRAM Doesn't Have
- **Manufacturing scale**: 85 employees; $55M revenue — 1/100th the scale of memory industry leaders
- **Multiple foundry sources**: GlobalFoundries-dependent for most production — single supplier risk
- **Pricing power vs. large fabs**: If Samsung/TSMC commercialize MRAM, they can price at marginal cost and overwhelm Everspin
- **Data center penetration**: No announced hyperscaler design win; data center TAM remains theoretical

### Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Business | Threat Level |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Avalanche Technology | Private STT-MRAM | Low (private, smaller) |
| Samsung (embedded MRAM) | Foundry MRAM for SoCs | Medium (different use case) |
| TSMC (MRAM research) | Embedded NVM | Low today, high in 3-5Y |
| Micron (3D XPoint successor) | Storage class memory | Low (different product) |

**Verdict: Narrow** — defensible in military/industrial niche for 3–5 years via certification barriers and IP; NOT wide moat because large fabs can enter and overwhelm with scale. Moat durability contingent on execution of US defense manufacturing and data center wins before large-cap entry.
