## 1. Critical Context

Everspin reports both **GAAP** and **non-GAAP** results. The gap between them is material:

- **SBC add-back**: ~$1.4M/quarter ($5.8M/FY2025) — excluded from non-GAAP
- **D&A add-back**: ~$700K/quarter ($3.2M/FY2025) — excluded from non-GAAP
- **"Other Non-Operating Income"**: $4.4M in FY2025; $2.0–2.1M in each of Q4 2025 and Q1 2026 — INCLUDED in both GAAP and non-GAAP. Source undisclosed (possibly DoD R&D grants, technology licensing, or pre-contract payments). This line is critical: it converts an operating loss into apparent profitability.

**Impact**: GAAP Q1 2026 = -$0.01 EPS; Non-GAAP Q1 2026 = +$0.11 EPS. Readers relying solely on non-GAAP figures without understanding the "Other Income" source are exposed to a material information gap.
