## Moat

### What COHR has
- **Scale in optical manufacturing**: One of only 3-4 companies globally capable of producing 800G/1.6T transceivers at hyperscaler volumes; manufacturing scale takes 10+ years to replicate
- **Customer qualification moats**: 12-18 month hyperscaler qualification process; switching mid-upgrade-cycle has very high cost
- **Compound semiconductor IP**: Deep InP, GaAs, and SiC expertise accumulated over 50+ years (II-VI founded 1971); critical for next-generation photonic ICs
- **Vertical integration**: Unlike pure fabless optical companies, COHR controls wafer fab, packaging, and testing — quality differentiation at scale

### What COHR doesn't have
- **Software-like margins**: Gross margin 35% vs software peers at 70-80%; manufacturing cost structure requires continuous CapEx ($440M/year)
- **Pricing power vs Chinese peers**: Chinese optical module makers (Innolight, Accelink, HiSilicon) compete aggressively on price in commodity segments
- **Balance sheet cleanliness**: $3.9B gross debt and $2.5B preferred from the $6.5B II-VI/Coherent merger create financial complexity and dilution drag

### Verdict
**Narrow moat, defensible for 2026-2028 AI buildout cycle.** Scale + qualification lock-in protects near-term revenue; not durable beyond 3 years without continued technical differentiation.
