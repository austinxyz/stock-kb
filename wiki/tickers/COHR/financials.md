## 1. Critical Context

**Merger accounting**: COHR's FY2023 is the first full year after the January 2022 acquisition of Coherent Inc. by II-VI. The merger created $4.5B in goodwill and $3.2B in intangible assets, generating ~$550M in annual D&A amortization — making GAAP net income and diluted EPS highly misleading. Track **non-GAAP (adjusted) EPS** and **free cash flow** as primary metrics.

**Preferred stock**: COHR issued $2.5B in convertible preferred stock (Series B) in 2023 to refinance merger debt. Annual preferred dividends of ~$130M are subtracted before calculating common stockholder net income, causing GAAP diluted EPS to appear deeply negative even when the company is operationally profitable.
