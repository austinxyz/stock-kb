The quarterly gross margins in FY2026 Q1–Q3 are 16–17%, suggesting real-time GM is lower than historical peak due to revenue mix (more distribution, Malaysia ramp costs).

**Concern**: Despite +124% Q3 revenue YoY, gross margin is NOT recovering proportionally. Q3 FY2026 gross margin = $2.55M / $16.5M = 15.5% — below FY2022–2025 levels. This is the most important metric to watch.
