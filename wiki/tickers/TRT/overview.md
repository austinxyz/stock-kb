## Moat

**What TRT has**
- Embedded testing labs adjacent to customers' fab/assembly operations in SE Asia (high relocation cost)
- Proprietary Burn-In Board (BIB) design and manufacturing capability
- 60+ years of industry relationships and automotive/aerospace certifications (AEC-Q100)
- First-mover in AI GPU reliability testing in Penang, Malaysia ecosystem

**What TRT doesn't have**
- Scale advantage vs. Amkor ($7B revenue) or ASE ($19B revenue)
- Software or data moat
- IP protection on BIB designs (replicable)
- Pricing power (pure-service model, margin-thin)

**Verdict**: Narrow moat. Relationship-based switching costs are real but fragile. A major OSAT building in-house testing capacity in Malaysia would materially impair TRT's position.
