## Moat

**Narrow — time-limited in a specific niche.**

### What CLFD has
- FieldSmart/FieldShield fiber management ecosystem (installed-base switching costs across 4,700+ ISP/CLEC customers)
- NOVA HD Panel: 384 LC / 4U at 0.2dB insertion loss — 50% better than peer specs in compact form factor
- CEO-level hyperscaler recognition as "superior expert" (Needham conf, May 2026)
- Opportunistic capital allocation: $50M+ buybacks at cycle low (FY24)

### What CLFD doesn't have
- Scale of Corning ($14B revenue, upstream glass integration)
- Software layer / network management (Calix advantage)
- International revenue (~100% North America limits TAM)

### Verdict
Narrow moat, durable for 3–5 years before larger players replicate NOVA specs. Must win named hyperscaler contracts within 18 months to establish data center credibility.
