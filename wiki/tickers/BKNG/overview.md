## Moat

**What BKNG has**: two-sided network (3M+ properties × 1.235B annual room nights); merchant payment rails + API integrations (supplier lock-in); 25-year EU SEO/brand moat ("Booking.com" is the verb); Agoda's Asia growth engine; $30B of 2021–25 buybacks + a first-time FY2024 dividend; merchant-model FCF conversion >1.5× net income.

**What BKNG doesn't have**: immunity from the discovery layer above OTAs (ChatGPT / Google AI Overviews / Perplexity); a US-revenue cushion (≈90% non-US → EUR/USD optics risk); a clean technical chart (see below — Stage 4 downtrend); a resolved near-term geopolitical overhang (Iran/Hormuz Q2 guide cut).

**Verdict**: 🟢 **Wide** — durable, multi-source moat compounded over 25+ years; the genuine tail risk is the AI discovery layer, not OTA peers (BKNG is the share-gainer vs EXPE/ABNB).
