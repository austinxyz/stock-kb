## Business in One Line

Pfizer is a global large-cap pharma ($142B market cap) rebuilding its revenue base after a $38B COVID windfall collapse, now betting on the Seagen oncology ADC platform to bridge the gap — while managing $64.7B in acquisition debt and a dividend that strains free cash flow.

## Moat

**What PFE has:**
- Global commercial infrastructure across 125+ countries (unmatched distribution depth)
- Seagen ADC platform: proprietary linker-payload chemistry across Padcev, Adcetris, Tukysa, Tivdak
- Regulatory relationships and FDA track record for large-molecule / combination approvals
- Scale advantages in manufacturing (biologics, vaccines, small molecules)

**What PFE doesn't have:**
- Durable patent protection on flagship revenue (Eliquis LOE 2026-2028, Vyndamax facing competition)
- Proven ADC commercialization at scale post-Seagen (integration still in progress)
- Growth story: forward EPS ($2.83 FY2027E) is BELOW current year ($2.94 FY2026E)
- Consumer brand moat: generics/biosimilars erode franchise revenue repeatedly

**Verdict: Narrow** — pipeline optionality via Seagen provides floor, but patent cliffs are a persistent headwind and ADC commercial ramp is unproven at scale. Moat duration: 3-5 year runway before meaningful erosion.
