## Moat

**Narrow / thesis-stage / regulatory-dependent.**

### What OKLO has
- Advanced reactor design IP: Aurora fast-spectrum metal-fueled reactor, passive safety, recyclable fuel
- DOE site authorization at INL: first-mover at Idaho National Laboratory pilot site
- $2.21B cash runway: largest balance sheet among non-utility SMR names (~11–15 yr at current burn)
- Hyperscaler ecosystem: Meta 1.2 GW Ohio prepayment + Switch 12 GW LOI + Equinix $25M prepay
- Sam Altman board chair (2.6% stake): AI-sector credibility with hyperscalers
- Kiewit Nuclear as construction partner (selected July 2025)
- **7/1 milestone**: DOE approved Final Documented Safety Analysis (DSA) for Groves reactor — clears last regulatory documentation hurdle before startup authorization

### What OKLO doesn't have
- Zero operating reactor; first commercial Aurora-INL target late 2027 / early 2028
- No NRC Combined License issued; COLA Phase 1 submitted, multi-year review ahead
- No firm revenue contracts: entire 14–18 GW pipeline is non-binding LOI
- Competitors with real milestones: Kairos Power (Google 500 MW binding deal), NuScale (NRC design approval)
- Burn accelerating: Q1 2026 op loss –$51.2M vs Q1 2025 –$17.9M (+186% YoY)

### Verdict
Narrow, thesis-stage moat. 7/1 DSA approval improves near-term Groves probability. Durable competitive position requires Aurora-INL commercial operation (late 2027).
