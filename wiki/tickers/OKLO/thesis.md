## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

**Raison d'être**: AI hyperscale datacenters need baseload carbon-free power at gigawatt scale within 3–5 years. The grid cannot deliver it. Solar/wind cannot provide 24×7 baseload. Natural gas triggers emissions commitments breaches. Existing nuclear fleet is fully contracted. **Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) are the only technology-ready answer** — but must survive regulatory, construction, and fuel-supply hurdles.

Oklo's **Aurora Powerhouse** is a 15–75 MW fast-spectrum liquid-metal-cooled reactor using HALEU fuel. Design features: passive safety, modular factory production, recyclable spent fuel, 20-year refueling cycle. Target customers: hyperscale datacenters, military/industrial sites, remote utility grids.

AI datacenter power demand alone growing at 25–30% CAGR; Goldman estimates data centers need 47 GW of incremental power by 2030. **End-market dynamics**:
- **Hyperscale datacenters** (META, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN): Primary target. Growing 30% CAGR in power demand; cannot rely on grid. Willing to pay premium for 24/7 carbon-free power. Risk: Microsoft Constellation nuclear deal (Crane Clean Energy, not SMR) shows large utilities compete.

- **International** (UK, Canada, Australia): Regulatory environment improving; but longer timelines and no current contracts.
- **Isotope production** (Groves): Niche but cash-flow-positive near-term; medical and industrial isotopes. Not a large market ($500M TAM) but validates technology.

**Competitive/pricing dynamic**: SMR economics face cost pressure from renewable-plus-storage ($40–60/MWh). OKLO's $110–130/MWh PPA targets only work if customers value reliability premium > $50–70/MWh. Current hyperscaler demand validates this premium thesis; risk is if batteries improve to $40/MWh at grid-scale by 2030.

**Policy tailwinds**: U.S. ADVANCE Act (2024) reduces NRC review timelines; CHIPS-adjacent nuclear policy support; IRA energy investment tax credits applicable to nuclear.
