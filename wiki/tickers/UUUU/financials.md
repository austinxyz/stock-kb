### Revenue Composition (FY2025 + Early 2026)

Revenue is driven primarily by **uranium delivery timing** under long-term
utility contracts. Each quarter's uranium revenue depends on contracted
delivery schedules which are set in advance. This makes quarterly revenue
volatile by design, not by operational failure.

REE (rare earth) carbonate revenue is a secondary and growing component:
- FY2023: First commercial REE sales
- FY2024–2025: Ongoing REE carbonate delivery to Neo Performance Materials
- FY2026+: REE SX plant builds toward separated REE oxide sales

HMS (heavy mineral sands) from Bass Metals acquisition (2024) added
ilmenite, rutile, zircon sales as a third revenue stream.

## Segment Financial Overview

### Uranium Segment (Primary)

| Metric | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Production rate | ~1.5M lbs U3O8/year |
| Key facility | White Mesa Mill, Blanding UT |
| Secondary facilities | Canyon mine AZ (restart mode), Nichols Ranch WY (standby) |
| Sales mechanism | Long-term utility contracts + spot sales |
| Approximate contracted price | ~$60–80/lb (vs spot $70–90/lb range) |
| Gross margin potential | 40–60% at $75+/lb price, controlled production cost |

### REE Segment (Growing)

| Metric | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Current product | Mixed REE carbonate (~25% NdPr, 45% La/Ce, other REE) |
| Feed source | Monazite from Bass Metals HMS (Brazil) + 3rd party |
| Current offtake | Neo Performance Materials (European separation) |
| SX plant status | Under construction; Phase 1 target: NdPr separation |
| Target product (post-SX) | Separated NdPr oxide, La oxide, Ce oxide (Phase 1) |
| Target product (Phase 2) | Heavy REEs: Tb, Dy |
| Revenue potential (SX operational) | $50–200M/yr depending on volumes and prices |

### Medical Isotope (Optionality)

| Metric | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Product | Radium-226 (Ra-226) |
| Source | Uranium processing byproduct at White Mesa |
| End market | Actinium-225 (Ac-225) production for TAT cancer therapy |
| Supply agreements | Signed (counterparty names not yet public) |
| Revenue (FY2025) | Pre-commercial / minimal |
| Revenue potential | $20–100M/yr if TAT market develops as projected |
| Unique moat | Only commercial-scale Ra-226 source from uranium processing |

### HMS / Bass Metals (New, Early)

| Metric | Status |
