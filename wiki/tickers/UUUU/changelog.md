### Strategic Thesis Summary

Three independent bull cases stack:

1. **Uranium**: White Mesa is the only operating conventional uranium mill
   in the US. Section 232 + Russian uranium import ban = structural tailwind.
   ~1.5M lbs/yr production + utility contracts at favorable prices.

2. **REE Independence**: Unique position processing monazite from own
   HMS operations (Brazil) through White Mesa to produce REE carbonate.
   REE SX plant under construction — will enable US-produced separated
   NdPr, La, Ce, and eventually Tb/Dy without Chinese dependency.

3. **Medical Isotopes (Radium-226)**: Only commercial-scale Ra-226 source
   from uranium processing; Ra-226 is precursor to Ac-225 for targeted
   alpha therapy (cancer treatment). Deeply asymmetric optionality — market
   is in early innings but supply agreements signed.
