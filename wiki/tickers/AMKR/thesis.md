## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Amkor exists because chips must be packaged — the silicon die alone is useless until it is connected, protected, and integrated into a system. As Moore's Law slows, the value increasingly shifts from transistor shrink to **advanced packaging**: chiplets, 2.5D/3D integration, and system-level packaging that stitch multiple dies together. Huawei's "韬定律" and the broader industry consensus now frame packaging — alongside power — as the key AI bottleneck. Amkor is the #2 OSAT (outsourced semiconductor assembly & test) globally, behind Taiwan's ASE, and is the primary US-listed pure-play on this wave.

The strategic catalyst: Amkor is building a **$2B advanced-packaging facility in Peoria, Arizona** (CHIPS Act-supported), positioning it to package chips for Apple and other US customers domestically — a geopolitical de-risking play as the US reshores advanced semiconductor supply chains.

**Milestone status (2026-06-01):**
- ✅ Stage 2 momentum leader: +314% from 52-wk low ($17.58) to $72.75; 7/7 trend template
- ✅ 4 consecutive earnings beats (Q4 2025 +56% surprise); EPS estimates rising hard (FY +19% in 90d)
- ✅ Revenue $7.07B (+27.5% YoY); gross margin improving (14% → ~18% in Q4)
- ⏳ Arizona Peoria fab: ramp timeline 2026–2027; CHIPS Act support
- ⚠️ Extension risk: MA200 +64%, near 52-wk high; analyst mean target $75.5 (+3.8% only)

**Pivotal Investment Question:**
> Will AI advanced-packaging demand (CoWoS / chiplet / 2.5D) drive Amkor's revenue + gross-margin expansion (14% → 18%+) sustainably enough to justify a 30x forward P/E — or has the +314% run already priced in a cyclical peak that mean-reverts once packaging capacity catches up?

## 4. Revenue Mix & Business Model

### 业务分部

| 分部 | 内容 | 趋势 |
|------|------|------|
| 通信（手机）| flip-chip / SiP，智能手机应用处理器 | 成熟，周期性 |
| 计算（AI/HPC）| 先进封装：2.5D/chiplet，AI GPU/加速器 | ↑ 高增长（AI 顺风核心）|
| 汽车/工业 | 功率/传感器封装 | 稳健 |
| 消费 | 通用封装 | 平稳 |

### 商业模式机制

Amkor 为**外包封装测试（OSAT）**：晶圆厂/无晶圆厂客户（NVDA、Apple、Qualcomm 等）将封装环节外包给 Amkor。Turnkey 模式（晶圆凸点 → 封装 → 测试 → 出货）。收入按封装服务计价 + 量。毛利率结构性低（14–19%，资本密集 + 商品化）；AI 先进封装是更高价值环节，是毛利率扩张的关键变量。

|------|------|------|
| ASE（ASX）| OSAT 第一，规模更大 | 高（直接竞争，近期略领先）|

- TSM CoWoS 内化：台积电自有先进封装抢高端 AI 封装份额

- OSAT 商品化：低毛利环节定价权弱

**Verdict: Narrow（窄护城河）** — OSAT 是关键但低毛利、资本密集、商品化环节。AI 先进封装稀缺性 + 亚利桑那卡位提供周期优势，但非 TSM/SNPS 级宽护城河。验证期：1–2 个 AI 封装周期。
