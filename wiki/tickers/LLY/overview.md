## Business in One Line

Eli Lilly is the world's leading GLP-1/incretin franchise — tirzepatide's superior dual GIP/GLP-1 mechanism dominates obesity + T2D markets while Foundayo (first oral GLP-1, approved April 2026) and a $50B+ manufacturing moat extend the competitive advantage for a decade.

## Moat

**What LLY has:**
- Tirzepatide patent portfolio (~20-year runway); dual GIP/GLP-1 mechanism IP
- $50B+ U.S. manufacturing commitment — capacity is a competitive weapon; no rival replicates in <5 years
- 150-year physician/endocrinologist relationships driving Mounjaro/Zepbound prescribing habits
- Foundayo (oral GLP-1): FDA approved April 1, launched April 9, 2026 — 12–24 months ahead of Viking VK2735 oral (Phase 3 positive, no filing) and Roche CT-388 (Phase 2)
- Pipeline: Retatrutide (triple agonist, Phase 3, 26.6% weight loss), Kisunla (Alzheimer's, approved 2024)

**What LLY doesn't have:**
- Diversification: ~61% revenue in tirzepatide is single-asset concentration risk
- Near-term FCF yield: CapEx $7.8B (FY2025) absorbs ~46% of OCF; normalizes as build peaks post-2027
- IRA pricing protection: tirzepatide (small molecule) eligible for CMS negotiation ~2028–2030

**Verdict:** Wide — manufacturing moat is most defensible differentiator for 3–5 years; franchise concentration is real but manageable given pipeline depth and long patent runway.
