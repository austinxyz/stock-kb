## Moat

### What QSI has
- Proprietary CMOS-based single-molecule semiconductor chip with patent protection
- First-mover advantage in commercial NGPS instrumentation (Platinum + Platinum Pro)
- Founder pedigree: Jonathan Rothberg created 454 Life Sciences and Ion Torrent (both acquired by industry leaders)

### What QSI doesn't have
- Ecosystem lock-in: low switching costs vs. Illumina's reagent/bioinformatics moat
- Revenue traction: sales declining in what should be the commercial growth phase
- Scale advantages: consumables margins require volume far above current throughput

### Verdict
**Narrow moat** — IP provides short-term protection, but unproven at commercial scale. Competitive window: 2 years to demonstrate platform differentiation before better-funded rivals close the gap.
