## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Proteins are the functional molecules of life — they execute virtually every biological process — yet sequencing them at single-molecule resolution has been technically impossible at accessible cost. DNA sequencing (pioneered by Illumina, PacBio, Oxford Nanopore) unlocked genomics; **protein sequencing is the analogous frontier for proteomics**. Quantum-Si's thesis: embed a single-molecule protein detector onto a semiconductor chip (CMOS), enabling massively parallel, real-time amino acid reading at a fraction of the cost of mass spectrometry — the incumbent method.

The commercial products — **Platinum** and **Platinum Pro** NGPS instruments — launched in 2022–2023. As of 2026-05-07, the company has generated $2.44M in FY2025 revenue (down from $3.06M in FY2024), burning $95M/year in cash. The platform has shipped to academic labs globally but has not yet demonstrated the volume adoption needed to validate a mass-market business model.

**Pivotal Investment Question:**
> **"Will Quantum-Si achieve $1.5M+ quarterly revenue by Q4 2027 — before its next mandatory equity raise — demonstrating that its NGPS platform has found product-market fit beyond niche academic research?"**

**Current milestone status:**
- Shipped: Platinum and Platinum Pro instruments; reagent kits; bioinformatics software
- Pending: Volume-scale commercial adoption; Pharma/CRO partnerships; next-gen chip generation
- Binary: Revenue inflection before cash depletion (~Q1 2027) or dilutive raise at likely unfavorable price

### Customer Engagement Stack

| Customer Type | Role | Status |
|--------------|------|--------|
| Academic research labs (global) | Early adopters, publications | Low volume; limited repeat orders |
| Pharma / CRO (pilot programs) | Target for scale | No material contracts announced |
| Clinical labs | Long-term target | Pre-validation stage |

### Business Model Mechanics

Capital equipment (instrument) + recurring consumables (reagent kits) — identical structure to Illumina's genomics model. At scale, recurring consumables provide the durable revenue stream; instruments are placed at marginal/break-even. Current reality: instrument placements are stagnant and consumable pull-through is insufficient to offset. Gross margins at 47% are structurally sound for scale; the challenge is denominator growth.

## 5. Competitive Moat

### Defensible Position
- **Semiconductor chip IP**: Proprietary CMOS-based single-molecule detector with patent moat (~3–5 year defensibility window)
- **Rothberg founder effect**: Brand credibility for academic/NIH grant-funded purchases
- **Early installed base**: Academic lab installations create reference site network (weak but real)

### Competitors by Business Line

| Competitor | Approach | Status | Threat Level |
|-----------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Nautilus Biotechnology | Cyclic sequencing proteins via antibodies | Private; raised $200M+ | High (direct) |
| Seer Bio (SEER) | Proteograph mass spec enhancement | Public; ~$180M mkt cap | Medium (adjacent) |
| PacBio (PACB) | Long-read DNA; exploring protein work | Public; ~$350M | Low (indirect) |
| Thermo Fisher / Waters | Mass spectrometry incumbents | Dominant | Medium (incumbent inertia) |

### What Is Eroding the Moat
- Nautilus well-funded and claims superior read accuracy
- Mass spectrometry incumbent advantages in regulatory-validated workflows (clinical)
- Low switching costs: protein sequencing has no Illumina-equivalent bioinformatics lock-in yet

### Verdict
**Narrow moat** — IP-protected but not ecosystem-defended. Verification deadline: 2027 (before next funding round). If revenue still declining in 2027, moat assessment = None.

## 12. BAIT Framework (Mauboussin)

### B — Behavioral
Small-cap biotech investors systematically overpay for technology narratives before revenue is proven. QSI benefited from 2021 SPAC euphoria and the "proteomics = next genomics" story. Market has now overcorrected to the bearish extreme — $0.98 price implies near-zero probability of commercial success despite $163M in cash. **Verdict: Slight behavioral edge for contrarians at current price — but only if thesis validates.**

### A — Analytical
Most analysts discount QSI on declining revenue without fully appreciating: (1) EV is essentially negative once cash is subtracted; (2) the proteomics TAM is real and large; (3) cost cuts improve the burn profile even if revenue is stagnant. However, the declining revenue trend is analytically damning — there is no model in which declining revenue at this scale is "good news." **Verdict: Analytical edge unclear — market may be correct.**

### I — Informational
Social media coverage is nearly zero. No institutional analyst spotlight. QSI operates in an information vacuum that creates potential for a large positive surprise if a major pharma partnership is announced. On the downside, lack of coverage also means bad news (dilution, compliance warning) gets no buy-side defense. **Verdict: Information edge minimal; negligible retail awareness.**

### T — Technical
Stage 4 with 2/7 trend template. Price at $0.98 is below MA150 (-22%) and MA200 (-23%), with MA200 declining. No valid SEPA entry exists. **Verdict: Avoid — worst technical profile in current wiki coverage.**
