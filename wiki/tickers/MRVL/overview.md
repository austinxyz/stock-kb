## Business in One Line

Marvell designs custom AI ASICs for hyperscalers (Google, Meta, Amazon) and high-speed networking semiconductors (SerDes, optical DSP, Ethernet switches) — the "picks and shovels" of AI data center infrastructure that sits one level below NVIDIA in the compute stack.

## Moat

**Narrow — defensible SerDes IP and design-cycle lock-in, but AVGO is a stronger rival.**

### What MRVL has
- **SerDes leadership**: 20+ years of high-speed serial interface IP — the hardest-to-replicate physical layer technology for 100G+ data rates
- **Custom ASIC design expertise**: Confirmed design wins at Google (TPU SerDes), Meta (MTIA), and Amazon (Trainium) — each win is a 3-5 year revenue stream
- **Optical DSP dominance**: Market leader in 800G coherent optical DSP chips used in data center interconnects
- **TSMC partnership**: Premium access to TSMC advanced nodes (N5/N3)

### What MRVL doesn't have
- **AVGO's breadth**: Broadcom serves more hyperscalers with a broader product portfolio; MRVL is #2
- **Own fab**: Fully fabless; dependent on TSMC for all manufacturing
- **CPU/GPU compute IP**: No in-house neural engine or matrix math block; relies on customer to define the compute architecture
- **Networking system integration**: Cisco, Arista have system-level relationships MRVL lacks

### Verdict
**Narrow moat.** SerDes IP and ASIC design relationships create meaningful switching costs over a 3-5 year design cycle. Verifiable time horizon: through FY2028 (current design wins in production). Post-FY2028 moat depends on next-generation design win rate.
