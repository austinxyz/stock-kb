## Critical Context — GAAP vs Non-GAAP

Marvell's GAAP financials are significantly distorted by two items:

1. **Acquisition amortization**: The $10B Inphi acquisition (April 2021) and $1.1B Innovium acquisition (October 2021) created ~$7–8B in acquisition-related intangibles, generating ~$1.3–1.5B/year in amortization charges. This crushes GAAP operating margin but is non-cash and one-time in nature.

2. **FY2026 Q3 one-time tax benefit**: A $1.9B deferred tax asset recognition was booked in Q3 FY2026 (ended November 2025). GAAP net income of $2.67B and EPS of $3.07 for FY2026 are materially elevated by this non-recurring item. Underlying recurring GAAP net income was approximately $770M.

Non-GAAP EPS (which excludes amortization, SBC, and restructuring charges) is the primary metric used by analysts and management. FY2027E non-GAAP EPS consensus: $3.83/share.
