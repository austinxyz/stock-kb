## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

On Holding AG is a Swiss premium athletic and lifestyle brand founded in Zurich in 2010 by Caspar Coppetti, David Allemann, and former Ironman triathlete Olivier Bernhard. The proprietary CloudTec cushioning system, combined with Swiss industrial-design aesthetics and a no-permanent-discounting retail policy, built a brand that competes at the top of the $140B global athletic footwear market alongside Nike, Hoka (Deckers), and Adidas — anchored by co-investor Roger Federer and a growing tennis/running athlete roster (Iga Świątek, Ben Shelton, Jack Draper).

**Strategic position as of 2026-08-11**: The company was riding a genuinely strong FY2025→Q1 2026 run — record 62.8% FY2025 gross margin, Q1 2026 gross margin 64.2%, guidance raised twice, and a rare $6.6M coordinated insider-buying cluster from all three co-founders on 2026-05-14. Today's Q2 2026 print broke that momentum on the top line: revenue CHF 850.3M (+21.6% cc) missed the CHF 881.4M consensus, and management cut full-year revenue growth guidance to "low-20s%" cc (from ≥23% cc) with a narrower FY sales range of CHF 3.47–3.56B. The stock fell -18.84% to a fresh 52-week-low territory ($31.48, vs. the prior 52-wk low of $31.41 set in March).

**Pivotal Investment Question**:

> **Was today's Q2 print a one-quarter deceleration inside an intact long-term compounding story — where margin expansion (GM 65.4%, +3.9pp) and DTC acceleration (+34.3% cc) prove the brand engine is still healthy — or is it the first confirmed data point of the "athletic footwear cycle peaking early" bear case that the prior analysis flagged as a 15%-probability tail risk?**

Current milestone status:
- ✅ Gross margin trajectory intact and improving (65.4% Q2, above the ≥64.5% FY guide floor)
- ✅ DTC channel accelerating (+34.3% cc), not decelerating — rules out the "wrong kind of DTC" thesis-break trigger from the prior analysis
- ❌ Constant-currency revenue growth guide cut for the first time in this company's public history (≥23%→"low-20s%")
- ⏳ Whether "low-20s%" stabilizes or continues decelerating is the open question — only one data point (today) exists so far

## 14. Bottom Line

**1-year view**: The immediate question is whether "low-20s%" cc growth holds through Q3 2026 (date TBD, ~November). A hold → stock likely re-rates back toward $40-45 as the panic fades. A further deceleration below 20% → stock likely retests or breaks the $30 low.

**3-year view**: Bull $70-90 (growth reaccelerates, DTC/apparel initiatives fully mature); Base $45-55 (steady "low-20s%→teens" grower, margin-led compounding); Bear $18-25 (footwear cycle genuinely peaking, Hoka/Nike share recapture confirmed).

**Portfolio allocation**: No position recommended today. This is a Watch, not an Initiate — see §11 Scenario B triggers.

**Verdict (2026-08-11)**: **Thesis Weakened, not Broken.** The core bull case (brand premium sustaining 60%+ gross margins) was reconfirmed today — margin hit a fresh record even through a growth miss. What broke was the ≥23% cc growth assumption underpinning the most optimistic scenarios. This is a real, negative, but not catastrophic data point. The stock's -18.84% reaction likely overshoots the actual fundamental delta, but "likely overshoots" is not the same as "definitely overshoots" — Watch for confirmation (Q3 growth rate, insider buying, analyst target resets, technical base formation) before treating this as a buying opportunity.
