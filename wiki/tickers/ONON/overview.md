## Moat

**What ONON has**
- Brand premium + no-permanent-discounting discipline sustaining 65%+ gross margin — rare for a growth-stage brand
- Athlete ecosystem (Federer co-investor, Świątek, Shelton) and Swiss design identity (CloudTec) as visual/brand differentiation
- DTC channel ownership (41.8% of FY2025 sales, Q2 DTC +34.3% cc) — higher margin, direct customer data

**What ONON doesn't have**
- No structural switching costs or network effects — moat requires continuous brand/product reinvestment
- Direct commoditization risk from Hoka (Deckers) at similar price points and accelerating in trail running
- Small revenue base (<2% global athletic footwear share) — the deceleration narrative that hit today is exactly the "is this brand's growth wall arriving early" bear case

**Verdict**: Narrow — durable pricing power evidenced by margin expansion even through today's growth miss, but no hard moat against Hoka/Nike encroachment. Today's print doesn't change the moat assessment; it tests the growth-rate assumption sitting on top of it.
