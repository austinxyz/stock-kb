## 1. Critical Context

ONON is a Swiss foreign private issuer filing Form 20-F (not 10-K). Reports in CHF; ~62% of revenue is USD-denominated (Americas), creating a persistent CHF-strength headwind on reported (vs. constant-currency) growth — always check which growth figure ("reported" vs. "cc") is being cited.
