## Business in One Line

Accenture is the world's largest IT-services and consulting platform (~$70B revenue, 786K employees), whose structural position as the orchestration layer for enterprise AI transformation either sustains its wide moat or gets commoditized by the very AI tools it deploys — the central investment question at a trough multiple.

## Moat

**What ACN has**
- 195/200 top clients retained 10+ years — embedded multi-year managed services; rip-and-replace extremely costly
- 786K employees across 52 countries — only firm capable of staffing multi-service, multi-geography AI reinvention at scale
- Top ecosystem partner with all major AI/cloud vendors (AWS, Azure, Google, OpenAI via Federal Services); partner-sourced revenue growing faster than the firm
- 30+ years transaction data enabling AI-powered pricing, fraud detection, listing quality

**What ACN doesn't have**
- No IP or proprietary product — services are replicable (just not at scale)
- No inventory/logistics moat; direct competitors (Deloitte, IBM, TCS, Infosys, Capgemini) all compete on each contract
- Headcount-based delivery is the most vulnerable to AI commoditization

**Verdict**: Wide moat — under pressure from the AI disruption threat the market is pricing in. Verification horizon: 4–8 quarters of bookings and book-to-bill data.
