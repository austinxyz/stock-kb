## Business in One Line

Meta owns the world's largest social media ecosystem (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Threads) with 3.4B daily active people, monetizing attention through the highest-ROAS digital advertising platform on earth.

## Moat

**What META has:**
- Network effects: 3.4B daily active people across FB/IG/WA/Threads — each new user increases value for all others
- Advertising targeting superiority: AI-enhanced ROAS outperforms all peers; advertiser switching cost is high
- Data flywheel: years of behavioral data build better ad models; impossible to replicate cold
- Infrastructure scale: Llama LLM backbone; private AI stack already deployed in production across all products

**What META doesn't have:**
- Enterprise revenue diversification (>97% advertising — single revenue stream)
- Search or cloud revenue to cushion social ad cycle downturns
- Proven AI monetization outside core ad targeting (Meta AI assistant still pre-revenue)

**Verdict:** Wide moat. Network effects + data flywheel are durable at 3.4B-user scale. TikTok is the only credible attention competitor, and even TikTok has not meaningfully dented META's ad pricing power.
