## 1. Why Does This Company Exist? + Pivotal Investment Question

Meta Platforms exists to connect people at global scale and monetize that connection through advertising. What began as a college social network has compounded into a 3.4-billion-person daily active user ecosystem spanning Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Threads — the largest human attention platform ever built. The strategic insight: whoever captures the most human attention and can target it precisely with relevant commercial messages captures an enormous portion of global advertising spend.

Today's session is critical context: Meta reported Q2 2026 earnings on July 29–30, 2026, missing EPS estimates ($6.18 actual vs $7.22 estimate, -14.4%). The stock fell -8.79% to $530.99. CEO Mark Zuckerberg went "on the offensive selling AI optimism" but provided limited concrete monetization timelines for AI products — hence the market reaction. The underlying concern: CapEx nearly doubled from $37.3B in FY2024 to $69.7B in FY2025, compressing FCF from $54.1B to $46.1B despite 22% revenue growth.

**Pivotal Investment Question:** Will META's AI infrastructure investment ($69.7B CapEx in FY2025, doubling from FY2024) translate into measurable ad revenue acceleration and new product monetization before free cash flow compression becomes a sustained structural concern — specifically, will FY2026 FCF recover above $55B?

The bull case hinges on AI delivering: (1) higher ad ROAS → pricing power → revenue growth above 25%, and (2) new revenue streams (Meta AI assistant, Llama API licensing, AR/VR) becoming material by 2027. The bear case: capex keeps rising, AI ROI is diffuse and slow-to-monetize, and the EPS miss pattern continues.

## 5. Competitive Moat

**Defensible advantages:**
- **Network effects (Wide):** 3.4B daily active people. Each user makes the platform more valuable to advertisers and to other users. Cold-start problem is insurmountable for new entrants.
- **Data flywheel:** Years of behavioral, social graph, and purchase intent data train better ad models. Google and Apple have device-level data; Meta has social graph + behavioral data — different and complementary.
- **Advertiser switching cost:** Ad campaign ROI is measurable, and META's ROAS consistently outperforms peers. Advertisers who optimize their funnels around META's tools face real switching friction.
- **AI infrastructure (emerging moat):** Llama, custom silicon (MTIA chips), private AI stack. If AI dramatically improves ad relevance, this compounds the existing moat.

**Competitors by business line:**

| Competitor | Business Line | Threat Level |
|------------|--------------|-------------|
| TikTok / ByteDance | Short-form video (Reels competitor) | High (attention) |
| Google / YouTube | Video advertising, search | Medium (different format) |
| Snap | Younger demographic | Low (much smaller scale) |
| Apple | App tracking transparency erosion | High (regulatory/structural) |
| LinkedIn (MSFT) | B2B advertising | Low (different use case) |

**Moat erosion risks:**
- Apple ATT (App Tracking Transparency) permanently reduced cross-app tracking. META adapted via on-device AI + Advantage+ campaigns, but the structural disadvantage vs pre-ATT era persists.
- EU DMA (Digital Markets Act) constrains data sharing across META's own apps in Europe — reducing targeting efficiency for ~21% of revenue.
- TikTok captures disproportionate youth attention. Reels is META's response, now $10B+ revenue run-rate, but at lower CPM.

**Verdict:** Wide moat. Network effects at 3.4B-user scale are durable. TikTok is the only existential attention competitor, and even then META's ad infrastructure superiority means it monetizes its users more efficiently. Moat is defensive, not expanding. Verify at: (1) sustained ARPU growth in US/Canada, (2) Advantage+ adoption rate among advertisers.

## 9. Industry-Specific Macro Analysis

**TAM:** Global digital advertising market ~$740B (2025), growing ~12% annually (eMarketer). META captures ~21% of global digital ad revenue — the second-largest share after Google.

**End-market dynamics:**

| Sub-Market | Trend | META Exposure |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| Social media advertising | +18% annually | Direct — FB/IG core |
| Short-form video advertising | +35% annually | Partial — Reels |
| Messaging-based commerce | +40% annually | Emerging — WA Business |
| AR/VR advertising | Pre-revenue | Optionality |

**Competitive/pricing dynamic:**
- Digital ad pricing (CPM) is cyclically sensitive. In a recession, CPM compresses significantly (2022 showed this). Macro is currently "good news is bad news" (GDP miss = rate hike concern eased = ad cycle risk unchanged).
- AI-driven efficiency (Advantage+) may structurally raise CPM floors by delivering better ROAS even in soft macro.

**Regulatory headwinds:**
- EU Digital Markets Act (DMA): Live since 2024. Limits data portability between META's own apps. Reduces targeting in EU (~21% of revenue).
- US FTC: Ongoing antitrust monitoring. Forced divestiture of IG/WA is the tail risk; settlement more likely.
- India data localization: Potential requirement to store Indian user data locally — significant WhatsApp compliance cost.
