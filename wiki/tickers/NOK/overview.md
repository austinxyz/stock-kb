## Business in One Line

Nokia designs and sells 5G/4G mobile networks, fiber/optical/IP infrastructure, and cloud network services globally — and licenses its ~20,000-patent portfolio (Nokia Technologies) as a high-margin recurring revenue stream.

## Moat

**What NOK has:**
- ~20,000-patent portfolio (Nokia Technologies) generating recurring licensing revenue from Apple, Samsung, and others
- Deep carrier relationships globally — high switching costs mid-deployment cycle
- Scale enabling $4.86B/year R&D investment that smaller rivals cannot match
- Standards body influence (founding 3GPP member for 5G specifications)

**What NOK doesn't have:**
- Pricing power in hardware (radio units commoditized; brutal Ericsson/Samsung competition)
- Software-level margins (still predominantly hardware-weighted)
- A clear moat in Open RAN (which disaggregates the traditional OEM model)

**Verdict: Narrow** — Patent portfolio and carrier relationships provide 3–5 year defensibility. Open RAN disaggregation is a structural long-term threat. Not a wide-moat business.
