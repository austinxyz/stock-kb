## 1. Critical Context — Bank Holding Company, Not an Industrial

Goldman Sachs is a **regulated bank holding company**. Traditional
industrial metrics (CapEx, FCF margin, "cash runway") are not the primary
lens here:
- **ROE, Book Value Per Share (BVPS), and Price/Book** are the primary
  valuation metrics
- **Efficiency ratio and IB/trading revenue mix** matter more than gross
  margin
- Capital adequacy is governed by **CET1 ratio and Fed stress-test
  requirements**, not a burn-rate/runway model
- FY2023 results were depressed by the **Marcus/consumer-banking wind-down**
  (real-estate and GreenSky impairments); FY2024–FY2025 and especially
  Q2 2026 reflect the post-wind-down recovery to core IB/trading/AWM
