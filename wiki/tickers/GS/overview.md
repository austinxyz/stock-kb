## Moat

**Wide moat in advisory/trading franchise; narrower in Asset & Wealth
Management.**

### What GS has
- **#1–2 global M&A advisory franchise** — decades of relationship capital
  with the largest corporates, PE sponsors, and sovereigns
- **Elite talent density** — self-reinforcing recruiting/retention edge
- **$1.8T balance sheet + regulatory capital scale** — a moat regional and
  boutique banks cannot replicate
- **Trading network effects** — deep liquidity provision attracts more
  flow, improving pricing further

### What GS doesn't have
- **Large retail deposit franchise** (unlike JPM/BAC) — deliberately
  exited consumer banking (Marcus, GreenSky) after it destroyed value
  2021–2023
- **Protection from deal-cycle cyclicality** — the moat protects market
  share, not revenue level; a dealmaking drought hits GS regardless of
  competitive position
- **Immunity from private-credit disintermediation** — Apollo/Ares/
  Blackstone Credit increasingly compete for financing mandates once
  routed through bank balance sheets

### Verdict
Durable competitive position in advisory/trading; the current record
quarter reflects **both** franchise strength **and** an unusually strong
point in the deal cycle — the two are not easily separated from the
outside.
