## Critical Context — Accounting Scandal Overhang

- **2024 delayed SEC filings**: SMCI failed to file its FY2024 10-K on time; required extension
- **Ernst & Young resignation**: E&Y resigned as auditor in October 2024, citing concerns it "was not willing to be associated with" SMCI's financial statements
- **BDO appointed**: New auditor BDO USA signed on; filed restated financials
- **SEC investigation resolved**: No material restatement required — most positive outcome possible from the crisis
- **Ongoing governance risk**: Market requires 2–4 years of clean filings to fully rebuild institutional trust

## Gross Margin Trend

| Period | Gross Margin | Commentary |
|--------|:------------:|------------|
| FY2022 | 21.7% | Peak margin; low AI server mix, higher-margin storage/networking dominant |
| FY2023 | 18.0% | Revenue doubles; AI server mix rises, pulling margin down |
| FY2024 | 13.8% | AI server becomes majority; H100 GPU servers thin margin by design |
| FY2025 | 11.1% | Scale without pricing power; component costs remain high |
| TTM (Q1 FY2026) | **8.4%** | Continued compression; GB200 ramp with aggressive pricing |
| Q1 FY2026 (Mar) | **~9.9%** | $1.02B gross profit / $10.2B revenue |
| Q2 FY2026 (Dec) | **~6.3%** | $798M / $12.7B — worst recent quarter |

**Trend**: Margin compressed from 21.7% (FY2022) to 8.4% (TTM) as AI server mix rose. Every new GPU platform forces SMCI to price aggressively to capture allocation from NVIDIA. Margin recovery to 12% requires either ASP increase (difficult) or component cost reduction (requires scale + NVIDIA to reduce GPU prices).
