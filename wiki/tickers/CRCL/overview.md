## Business in One Line

Circle issues USDC, the #2 global USD stablecoin (behind Tether),
earning revenue primarily as interest on the Treasury reserves backing
it in circulation — economically closer to a rate-sensitive
money-market manager than a traditional payments company.

## Asset Type

growing, the balance sheet is clean (net cash, FCF-positive), but the

post-IPO boom-bust cycle (IPO $31 → $263 close in 3 weeks → $50 low →

## Moat

### What CRCL has
- **#1 US-regulated/GENIUS-Act-compliant stablecoin issuer**, #2
  globally by circulation behind Tether
- Conservatively-managed reserves (BlackRock-managed Circle Reserve
  Fund) — no history of reserve mismanagement
- Clean, debt-free, cash-generative balance sheet
- Early positioning in cross-border payments (Circle Payments Network)
  and institutional settlement infrastructure (Arc blockchain)

### What CRCL doesn't have
- **Pricing power over its own core revenue** — Coinbase and Binance,
  who custody most circulating USDC, capture a large and rising share
  of reserve income via distribution agreements
- Tether's scale and offshore/EM distribution dominance
- A proprietary technology moat — fully-reserved stablecoins are
  relatively commoditized at the technical level
- Defense against the Fed rate-cutting cycle, which directly compresses
  its core revenue line

### Verdict
**Narrow, eroding moat.** Regulatory/brand leadership is real but the
economics are controlled more by distribution partners than by Circle
itself — the gross margin trend is the tell.
