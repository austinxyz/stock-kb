### 🔴 Gross Margin Collapse (FY2022–FY2025)

| FY | Gross Margin | Commentary |
|----|:------------:|------------|
| 2022 | 32.4% | Baseline, pre-IPO |
| 2023 | 29.4% | Slight compression begins |

| 2025 | **8.7%** | **Four straight years of decline** — the central risk in this thesis |

**This is the most important data point in the entire analysis.**
Revenue nearly quadrupled FY2022→FY2025, but gross profit is *lower in
dollar terms* in FY2025 ($238.1M) than FY2022 ($249.7M) — all the
incremental revenue growth is flowing through as distribution cost to
Coinbase/Binance, not to Circle's bottom line.
